Krishna Devangamath, Art: Muralidhar Rathod

One hundred sown, one will sprout
The spark will leap out and push itself out


A friend, male or female, who remains even beyond death for loneliness
The wonder of a relationship that sometimes becomes everything
The more it fails, the more it yearns, remaining unquenched
The melody of unfulfilled emotion always emerges


The expanse of the soul laid bare, seeing what was opened
The way she sits on the lap and descends into depth
A spring of wonder that takes root and spreads


Maturing in silence, swimming in eddies
Learning to untie the knots of Brahma, one after another
A devout vow of meditation without sitting in Padmasana


It is possible to tie up and rebuild those who broke and built
It is possible to light a lamp from the fire of pain
To swim effortlessly like a fish
