Roopashree Kalliganur

Raghunatha Cha Ha

Phoenix Ravi

Phoenix Ravi, Art: Roopashree Kalliganur

Poetry is;
Letters joining together, sparking into words
Words now become meaning.
Words joining words, like a string of firecrackers set alight
Let loose in the mind
A fire that burns and burns, crawling towards its goal
Or energy...!


The total meaning is somewhere far away;
Like a child who lost its mother in a fair,
Every letter, word, and sentence wailing in the darkness
Like seeing the mother in a flash of lightning, stumbling forward
As if an iceberg melted and suddenly plunged into the depths of water
Swimming, following the scent of the mother's womb
The mother might be somewhere far away
Burning, burning, at the end of the poem
Reaching the very last word, the meaning, a spark
Like launching a rocket, the mother suddenly standing in front of you in your inner self...!!
Or poetry...!!


It's just like sitting on a floating crocodile,
Mistaking it for a rock...!!
Having sat down, without directions
Without left or right
Without up or down
Without a middle
Where meanings break their chests and die...!!
