How much to read a dream
What to write a dream

Are being released
One by one, the bricks of the built house
The tiles are flying like birds
Flowing like a river
The beams and pillars,
How to stop the calamity
To wake up quickly,
Sleep disappeared
A dream sucking at sleep's breast

To the old woman
A series of youthful boons
A saree worn by a dream
Adorned with a golden armlet and a chintaku
Exactly like an apsara
Surrounded by
Sixteen thousand gopalas
The confused flute's buzzing sound
The dream lost itself

The surrounding walls are growing
Making the high sky and earth one
The roof is stumbling
The windows are closed
The night is running far away, waving its hand
The blood-sucking light is approaching

The life of the pen is over
The foundation of the dream has fallen apart
Must run again, picking it up
Chasing today, tomorrow, and the day after
