Houses embrace the rain
from the courtyard, from the leaking roofs
reaching out


The tree embraces the road
bending slightly where it stands
shedding night flowers and leaves


The moon embraces the earth
on a full moon night
making the ocean waves overflow


Tired of running and running, standing in one place

The clouds embrace each other

Then it rains


When embraced, the body feels light

The warmth of the bodies joining together

The body becomes even warmer


Though it may seem contradictory

Staying away is bondage

Embracing is liberation.
