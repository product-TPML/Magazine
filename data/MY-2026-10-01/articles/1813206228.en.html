When you are near

padya pallava_adiga.jpeg

Art: S.V. Hoogar

Gopalakrishna Adiga, Art; S.V. Hoogar

When you are near, like a lotus in the heart,
like a lung, the silent flowing blood in the veins
is an unconscious circulation. Between coffee,
snacks, and meals at appointed times, the heat that flares up
causes a commotion, a great upheaval;
when blood pressure rises, heart beats flutter
fever rises, and the forehead turns red hot,
your knowledge is only for words that pop like corn, you are the goal.

When the fever subsides and small leaves begin to fall
on the lake as usual, these two
neighboring boats drift apart on their own
and wait with white sails side by side
in the sun, in the evening glow, in the twists and turns of the night.
On parallel lines,
what dreams, what fruits of karma, what posture, what emotion, what torment
in the depths of the veins—if asked, unutterable, even if told,
unfathomable hooks, twists, turns,
doubts, fears, anxieties. Between words and minds,
what kind of tensions; the huge, nameless, ancient
twilight shadows; the new scratches of a tiger's
claws that tear through the sleeping faces and become vocal.

But all this is bearable; or the solid base of the pot of circumstantial boiling
is the constant, undisturbed foundation; a flute that always holds the pitch
and carries the rhythm of the original beat, even when it misses a beat or jumps
from one beat to another. As long as the gourd is being polished, the danger of it losing its balance and sinking to become just
a gourd is impossible. The radiant power of the circumference that brings satisfaction to the countless
radii formed in the center of the heart, salutations.
