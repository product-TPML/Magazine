Navilugannadi October 2026 (August Issue)

Manjushree M Kadakola

Aug My 2026

The interview with author and publisher B.N. Sriram in August Mayura was excellent. (Editor: J.Na. Tejashree) Sriram's statement that a movement is not possible due to the strengthening caste system and increasing superstition today is timely. The rulers and the people lack ideals, leading to a leadership vacuum. The increasing hatred is the reason for this. Sriram's words on movements, organizations, reading, the influence of leaders, and technological change are valuable.

M.G. Rangaswamy, Hiriyur

S. Divakar's story 'Ishwara' felt like it transported one to Kafka's absurd world. Along with that, the concept of existentialism deeply resonated in the story. The character of Ishwara, the protagonist, who has taken the form of past and future, is unforgettable in the reader's mind.

N. Shankar, Mysuru

C.S. Sarvamangala's article 'Kannada's Great Mind' published in Mayura Vishesha beautifully introduced T.S. Venkannayya. The beautiful portrait, reminiscent of Buddha's peaceful form, Bahubali's gentle smile, and firm resolve, captivated the mind. Although Venkannayya wrote less, his contribution to shaping great intellectuals like Kuvempu and G.S.S. is immense. Seeing the picture of the stalwarts of that era filled my heart.

K.P. Ramagundi Bailahongala, Venkatesh Machakanur Dharwad, Keshavaprabhu Shivamogga, Ba.Ram. Suresh, Tumakuru

The story 'Mrigajala' (Nagamangala Krishnamurthy) separated readers from the outside world, took them somewhere else, made them read, and lightened their minds and bodies. The charm of the village and the description of its nature came out naturally. When the story ended, sorrow welled up in my heart, and I cried.

Heggappura Raghavendra Kulkarni, Hosapete, Ballenahalli Manjunath, K.R. Pete

In the 'Kadige Kannu' series, which concluded in the August issue, Kaleemulla's writings, shared with his unique experience, transported us to another world. His distinct and intimate writing style made us look forward to it every month. Thanks to Kaleemulla and the 'Mayura' team for publishing this series.

N. Anand Rao, Mysuru

Meena Mysuru's experiential narrative 'Pathakke Hoguvudendare' was delightful.

Gurulingappa E. Biradar, Vijayapura
