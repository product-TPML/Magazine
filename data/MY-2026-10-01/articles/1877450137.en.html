Terli N. Shekhar Art: Muralidhar Rathod

If you nurture me
let it be amidst your thorns!

If there aren't enough thorns
in your garden for that
then crush and kill me;
nip the weed of love
in the bud.

Let my breath
germinate in your conch;
my thirst
in your eyes
my hunger
feed in your vastness.

How many times have we
become leaves, flowers, fruits
and bloomed
on the crumpled plant
lying on the bedsheets?

You, however, used to call
each tree and creeper by name
and put them to sleep after feeding them, didn't you?
How beautifully did you bid farewell to me,
who had taken root within you as a seed?

For your sleep
I will name it a forest
Our love
is the hissing within that forest cave.
Here I am
I will hide my 'Mohana Murali'
towards the dream-melody.
Love is a wondrous island
that can rain water where fire is needed
and fire where water is needed.
To cross its path
we must become solitary, we must become solitary
until it becomes possible within us –
that ultimate mantra of becoming solitary
if it is to be nurtured
let it be amidst your thorns.

Thorns –
they are the spring forms of sharpness.
