Telugu: G. Uma Maheshwar Kannada: Ranganath Ramachandrarao Art: Srujan

G. Umamaheshwar, a renowned Telugu storyteller, is an employee of Tata Consultancy Services. Many of his stories have been translated into Kannada and Tamil. 'Parayollu', 'Barosa' and 'Nemalikaalu Nemaruvetalu' are his popular collections of stories.

Ranganath Ramachandrarao, also known in Kannada literature, is a Telugu writer and translator. For the Central Sahitya Akademi, he has translated works from Kannada to Telugu such as 'Bandaya', 'Kallu Karaguva Samaya', 'Aramane', 'Om Namo', 'Halla Bantu Halla', 'Avadheshwari', Poornachandra Tejaswi's life and writings. He has received many awards and honors for his command of both languages.
