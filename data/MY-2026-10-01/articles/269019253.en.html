Puttan's Wish

Manjushree Kadakola

Art: Santhosh Sasihithlu

B.S. Shivakumar, Art: Santhosh Sasihithlu

A cherry fruit sits
on top of the ice cream.
The eyes that saw the red fruit
were drawn to it.

Someone brought it slowly
on a tray.
The little one, who is waiting,
is eager to eat it now.

He saw a hundred different colors
among what was there.
As the craving to eat bothered him,
he ate until his stomach was full.

He set out with his father
to go around the market.
Looking here and there,
he walked through the streets of the town.

Puttan, who insisted
on buying a shirt and pants,
raided the shop
and brought back a full bag.
