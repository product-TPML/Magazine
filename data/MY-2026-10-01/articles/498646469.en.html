Subbu Holeyar, Art: Vishwanath A.S.

In this search

I'm tired, I want to hide somewhere

where no one can see me anymore.

As long as they saw him

they insulted him, suspected him

in these days and in all centuries

he hid more.

He lived more in fear, hiding his face

so as not to show himself.

He has become even tougher now

everyone is talking

about him, about religion

they are asking in a loud voice

what is your name?

I spat. That's all!

When a three-fingered man slapped me

tears fell to my feet

I cursed his hand to turn to stone.

More and more people are standing

against him like water, like the waves

of the sea, rising and crashing.

They have stopped the guns

so that we don't hide in corners

in streets, in open spaces

enough police... soldiers to bind the earth.

He knows that we

come from ant hills

that we sprouted between Bhima's fingers

that we blossomed in letters.

When we were children, the game of hide-and-seek

what an innocent game, he thought

now he is trying to suppress

our very breath..

Without words

I felt his fingers on my cheek

I can't describe it in words

they were harsh, they were cruel.

A stubborn man, colder than a hundred minus degrees

he builds artificial ice rooms

and plays a game of collecting bodies

and making them live and die

he is a weak god

his temple is locked

not even a lamp is burning.

Our fists are clenched now

not to punch the air;

no more talk of defeat:

let's bring down the sky

we will raise blue flags

we, the citizens of India...!
