La

To sow means,
to make the world a child,
put it in a cradle
and swing it.


To sow means,
to touch dry hearts with electricity
and awaken them.


To sow means,
to be the in-laws of the clouds that have no front or back,
of the rain that pours here and doesn't pour there!


To sow means,
to be close to reality,
to kill countless doubts
and become a child playing on its mother's lap!


To sow means,
to make hunger a pet dog,
wash the face of consciousness
and send it out to play in the yard!

To sow means,
to become trust itself,
to be the ferryman who takes the boat to the shore,
to protect the light on the human face!

–Ashoka Surapura

Together

Life is not solitary, here

we must push forward together;

it is our nature

to live by trusting the land...

One by one, seeds must be buried in the soil; to get a harvest for life!

The drop of sweat that falls on the ground will not be in vain

The seed will sprout today or tomorrow, definitely...

Here there is no gender distinction between male and female
There is one spring for the living beings together
The time when affection gathers

The breath for the harvest is a meaningful name...

The strength of working hands will not go back; determination too must smile

Like a green harvest; then life

will flow like a river...

–Shi. Ka. Badiger, Bhagyanagar
