The pinnacle of friendship

Gunda used to go to the bar every day, drink two bottles of liquor, and then quietly walk away.

One day the waiter asked, 'Sir, you drink two bottles every day, your liver will be damaged!'

Then Gunda said, 'Listen, my friend, we were two friends. Each of us used to drink one bottle. Now my friend has passed away. I am drinking one bottle in his name.'

After a few days, Gunda started leaving after drinking only one bottle. Then the waiter, surprised, asked, 'What happened, sir, you are drinking only one bottle now?'

Gunda clarified, 'From today, I have stopped drinking!'

Raman Shetty Renjala

Let's see if you can tell the name

Once, when a teacher asked her students, 'Children, tell me what my name is?', a mischievous boy said, 'Madam, your name is 'Koli' (chicken/zero).'

When the madam, with a little surprise and a little anger, asked, 'Why is that?', the boy said, 'Because you always give everyone a chicken egg (zero) in math!' Then the teacher, with a slight smile, affectionately scolded him, 'You donkey!'

V. Vijayendra Rao

Who pushed?

Once, the boss organized a competition for all the workers in his company. There was a pond of water with dozens of crocodiles. The rule was that whoever crossed from one side to the other alive would get 50 lakh rupees as a prize. If someone died in the middle due to a crocodile, 10 lakh rupees would go to their heirs.

The office staff stood and watched, but no one entered the pond. Just then, one person jumped in. He somehow struggled through the crocodiles in the pond and reached the other bank. Everyone congratulated him.

But then that person shouted, 'Who pushed me?' Then his wife said, 'I thought the offer was good: 50 lakh rupees if he wins, 10 lakh rupees if he loses. So I pushed him.'

Bharathi Gangadhar

Not hungry, sir...

In the classroom, the math teacher was teaching. He wrote a problem on the blackboard, instructed the students to solve it, and sat on a chair.

Ten minutes later, when he checked all the students, everyone except Gunda in the back bench had solved the problem.

The angry teacher scolded, 'Gunda, no matter how many times I tell you, you don't understand! Pay a little attention to your studies. Don't you feel ashamed that all the teachers scold you? A person should have the hunger to learn and understand.'

To that, Gunda said, 'Not hungry, sir. I came after eating a full meal, sir!' and the math teacher was exhausted.

Chandrakumar D.
