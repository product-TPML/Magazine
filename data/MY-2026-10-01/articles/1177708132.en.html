Ramya was ready to go to school. Today was Wednesday. She knew she could wear any color dress she wanted. Uniforms were not compulsory on Wednesdays at her school. It could be said that Sujatha teacher, out of love for the children, made it so that children could celebrate one day a week by wearing clothes they liked. Last week, Ramya wore a new red frock that her father had brought. She stood in front of the mirror. How beautiful the pretty flower in front looked. Tiny golden dots sparkled all over her body. She was very happy. That's when she remembered. Her father had brought this frock last week, and the week before that he had also brought a red frock. It was similar to this one. But when she tried it on, it was too big for her. So her father had said he would return it and bring another one. But her mother said not to return it. "Our Ramya will grow up next year. Let her keep it. It's nice, bring another one," she had said. So her father brought this frock...

Shilpa's house is a little distance from Ramya's house. Her house is not a big slab house like Ramya's. It's a small hut with a tin roof. Both Shilpa and Ramya go to the government school in that village. Ramya has immense affection for Shilpa. Shilpa is smart in studies and quick in games. Ramya is always with Shilpa. Even when playing, she joins her team. Everyone knows that the team Shilpa is in usually wins the game. As soon as her father brought this dress, Ramya remembered Shilpa. She felt that she should give the dress her father had brought first to Shilpa. Shilpa's parents work daily in the gardens and fields of some distant house. It can be said that she has no other clothes apart from her school uniform. Even if she has one or two, they are torn or damaged in places. Ramya thought again, "If Shilpa and I both wear the same kind of frock to school, everyone will be happy to see us. Even though Shilpa is in my class, she is taller than me. The dress that is big for me will fit her perfectly."

The day after the second dress was brought, Ramya asked her parents, "Shall we give this dress to Shilpa?" Then her mother said, "No, no, you don't need to be a generous donor by giving her a new frock. Your father is never at home, always busy with the village school, temple, youth club, and cleanliness work. I'm fed up. You also don't need to go around helping others like your father," she said at length. Ramya was proud that everyone in the village loved her father. She remembered Sujatha teacher also saying, "Your father is a very good person, he helps with any school work." Her father had only said, "Ramya, let's see, don't rush. If we can give it, we will." After that, when Ramya mentioned giving the same frock a couple more times, her mother said, "Don't keep repeating the same thing, shut up," and silenced Ramya.

As a last resort, she wanted to ask again. The beautiful image of Shilpa standing in front of her, wearing a frock similar to hers, came to her mind and thrilled her. "Dad, shall I take that new frock and give it to Shilpa? She is my friend. She brings me guava, mango, and jamun fruits from somewhere. They had grown two pineapple plants in front of their house. Two had grown big. They gave one to us and only ate one themselves," she began to say. "Enough, how many stories she uses to justify," her mother said, but before she could finish... "She has been saying all this since that day, Shilpa is also a good girl. Let her give it," her father said, bringing the new frock and placing it in Ramya's hand. "Father and daughter, do whatever you want and get ruined," her mother said, dropping a couple of utensils with a clang and holding the end of her saree, she went to the kitchen.

Ramya didn't know what to do.
"Ramya, you take it. I will pacify your mother," her father said, sending her on her way to school. Ramya felt a kind of embarrassment from her mother's anger, but she ran with the joy that she could give it to Shilpa.

If you cross Ramya's garden and walk a little distance on a small road, you reach Janata Keri. The first tin house you see there is Shilpa's. Standing in front of the house, she called out loudly, "Shilpa... Shilpa." Her mother came out and said, "Shilpa has a fever and is sleeping. She won't come to school today. You go. I am also leaving for work," and got ready to leave. "I have brought a new frock to give to Shilpa," Ramya said. "What, a new frock? Where did you get it from, did you tell at home and bring it, or did you just bring it? We are poor. In the end, they might say something..." she began to say.

"Father himself gave it. I brought it to give to her with love," Ramya said. "Okay, she is sleeping now. Give it to her tomorrow after her fever subsides. She is sleeping now," said Shilpa's mother.

Ramya felt that it should be so. She put the frock in her bag and walked to school. Sujatha teacher and all her school friends were happy to see Ramya's dress and hugged her. Time passed. She left school and started walking back home.

When she came near Shilpa's house, she remembered her. She thought she would talk to her and then go, so she went near the door of the house and called Shilpa. The door was slightly open. At that time, all the adults would have gone out to work. When Shilpa didn't speak even after calling two or three times, she pushed the door open and looked inside. She saw Shilpa sleeping. She went closer and called her. But she was not speaking. She was sleeping with her eyes closed. There was only a pot of water and a glass near the mat. And when she called loudly and touched her head,
her hand was so hot that it burned. Shilpa only said "Hmm"
a couple of times and did nothing else. She didn't even move.

Ramya got scared. She didn't know where Shilpa's parents had gone. What to do... when she thought about it, she remembered Sujatha teacher. She ran towards the teacher's house. The teacher had just reached home. Ramya told her everything in one breath. Immediately, Sujatha teacher came with Ramya. They called someone and talked.

In a little while, an ambulance arrived. By then, Shilpa's parents had also arrived. "I learned everything from Ramya. Did you leave your daughter with a fever and go to work?" Sujatha teacher said, "Let's go to the hospital." They put Shilpa in the ambulance and left for the hospital. Sujatha teacher told Ramya to go home and she also left for the hospital.

Ramya ran home. "What, Rani, so late? You must have had a big celebration after giving the dress," her mother was saying... "Mom, I didn't give her the dress. Look, it's right here," she said, shedding tears and taking the frock out of the bag, her mother stood looking at it with a kind of
dismay.
