Appoint a beautiful nurse!

The old man, around 70, was admitted to the hospital for treatment. When the doctor came to see him, he said, 'Sir, I have a request. No matter how much money it costs for my treatment, I don't mind. I am ready to pay. Please appoint a beautiful nurse until my treatment is over and I return home healthy.'

The doctor was surprised and said, 'Uncle, what is this at your age... I don't understand.' In response, the old man smiled and said, 'Please don't misunderstand. I have two sons. If they know that there is a beautiful nurse to look after me, they will come to me in shifts three times a day and inquire about my well-being.' The doctor couldn't help but laugh.

Aravind G. Joshi

Shut up

Recently, for a kitty party, each of us had prepared a dish and gathered at our friend Vishala's house. As our friend Veena removed all the lids from the containers on the table and arranged them neatly, ready to take a photo, another friend shouted, 'Hey Veena, don't open your mouth..., shut it, shut it!' We all looked at her in astonishment.

When Veena also said with a little anger, 'I'm taking the photo with my mouth shut,' our friend realized the situation and said, 'What I meant was that my huggi (a dish) won't taste good if it gets cold, close the container lid.' The atmosphere cleared, and everyone started laughing.

Nalini T. Bhimappa

False!

This incident happened when my granddaughter Swara was in the first grade. It was my granddaughter's exam time. Since my son was going to the office, I used to help her with her school homework and studies. That evening, my son came home from the office, caressed his daughter, and asked, 'Shall we go see the falls?'

Then my granddaughter sadly said no. My son, who didn't expect this, asked, 'No? Why?'

'Hmm hmm, no, Grandpa has been teaching me that until now,' said my granddaughter.

When my son looked at me, not understanding anything, I, grasping the situation, laughed and said, 'Swara, your dad is calling you to the waterfalls. Not the true or false of the exam.' When she understood the truth, everyone present laughed heartily.

Sudheendra A. Kulkarni

Earbuds story

An incident that happened during a recent bus journey. I was sitting, listening to music with Bluetooth earbuds in my ears. An elderly person came and sat in the seat next to me. He was observing me. My stop was about to arrive. I took out the earbuds, put them in their case, and put them in my bag.

The elder asked, 'How much did you buy these for, son?' I said, 'Eight hundred.' He asked, 'Do they sound good?' I said, 'Yes.' He then showed me his hearing aid and said, 'Then why doesn't the one you gave me sound good? Please check and tell me, this isn't sounding right.'

I said, 'I don't know, Grandpa, get it checked at the hospital.' He said, 'But you had both in your ears.' I said, 'Those are different, these are different, Grandpa,' and got off the bus.

Narasimhareddy Yankamol
