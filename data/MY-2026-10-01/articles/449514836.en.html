Gandhi who came in a dream

Soorya V. Narayana

Bidaloti Ranganath

Bidaloti Ranganath, Art: Santhosh Sasihitlu

Gandhi Jayanti in the morning
In the half-dream of the night
My grandfather Rangappa looked like Gandhi
This man, full of anger and resentment
It's doubtful if he will become Gandhi


The lantern was burning
In the bag hanging on the wall nail
Tomorrow's life was there
In the soot of the beedi thrown by grandfather
Experience was solidified
Gandhi's shadow swayed over the shed swept in the evening


Grandfather had no regard for religion or caste
Even though he carried poor bones
He would stand holding the dreams of the hut
Giving shoulder to the bier of the dead
Wiping away tears


Grandfather
Did not chant peace mantras like Gandhi
To fill the stomachs of the hungry
He would give a seer and a quarter of ragi
And was known for spreading the light of the full moon on his face...

In the sandals sewn by grandfather
Dreams truly played
The path Ambedkar walked
Was also in his eyes
But he did not dream of Ramarajya like Gandhi
He was a medicine for the hands that cried out!


Climbing hills and mountains
Crossing forests and fields
Cutting firewood and filling his stomach
If Gandhi wove cloth on the charkha
These people sewed and wore torn clothes
And walked with their heads held high...


I did not see Gandhi
Even if he carried me on his shoulders and let my two legs hang
He would walk for miles without complaining
My grandfather's love and self-confidence
Surpassed even Gandhi's
