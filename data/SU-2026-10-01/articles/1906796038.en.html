Stepping into 'Times Square', the central hub of New York, felt like descending onto Brigade Road in our Bengaluru. It is located in 'Midtown Manhattan' in New York. This place, known as 'The Crossroads of the World', is always bustling and is a hub of commerce and entertainment. It houses Broadway theaters, skyscrapers, and is famous for public art events. This is called 'Midnight Moment'.

Its famous 'New Year's Eve Ball Drop Ceremony' is watched by people all over the world. Times Square is a symbol of New York's vitality and culture.

The main attraction here is the activities that go on past midnight. So, after dinner, we too descended onto the glittering streets after 11 PM. Digital billboards soared sky-high everywhere. In the dazzling colorful light, women walked with a cat-walk like grace. Shops, various restaurants, and food carts were abundant on both sides. There were also Indian restaurants.

People holding colorful parrots and birds for photos, magic shows, and music bands all stood along the roadside. It was like the festive atmosphere of our local fair. A group of women dancing to the rhythm of Korean music seemed like a new world. A wondrous magical world is created in Times Square, this magical city of New York. That's why even at two in the morning, people who don't feel like going home wander in their own world. Times Square is not just for locals but a hot spot for all tourists.

Broadway Show: The world-famous Broadway Show takes place on Broadway Street, near Times Square. To watch this show, tickets must be booked online in advance. The price ranges from 20 to 150 dollars and doubles on weekends. Various shows are held here.

The Lion King, Hamilton, Wicked, plays like Harry Potter,
The Outsiders are performed at different times. We went to a musical play called 'Aladdin'. In a well-equipped hall, there were seats for about 500 people, arranged in an ascending order so that everyone could watch without obstruction. In this two-and-a-half-hour musical drama, the costumes of the characters, the stage setting, and the sound effects all seemed to transport the audience to some magical world. g
