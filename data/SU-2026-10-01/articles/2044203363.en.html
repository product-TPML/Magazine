'Dream of building a curious narrative'

Raghavendra K.

What could be the reason for the saying 'Mane Magalu Swara'?

Those who have a daughter at home will quickly connect with the character of Swara. Swara has grown up beautifully. A brave girl marries a boy she doesn't like. The strange thing is that she has to live in the house of the boy she loves. Even in such distress, she surrenders to silence. Her character and nature resonate with the feelings of parents who have daughters. I also really like my father here. My father is the reason I agreed to a marriage I didn't like. This is a matter of honor, they say, marry this boy. Out of respect and love for my father, I will marry Bhuvan as he said. Many girls might be like this. This is an example of the delicate bond between a father and daughter. Couples who wish to have a daughter will also like this character.

Is the wedding celebration essential to the story?

Marriage is a central element in serials. In most serials, weddings are celebrated grandly. It is natural for everyone of marriageable age to desire marriage. Some even dream of it happening in a certain way. For that, they plan years in advance. After the engagement, there is a desire for a particular saree. Parents also struggle to marry their daughter according to her wishes. The story on screen happens just like real-life events. The grand wedding did not take place in 'Ananda Nilaya'. Even then, the auspicious event happened according to the needs of a middle-class family. The serial shows the desire for marriage.

Didn't anyone ask about the saree-dress?

That's very natural. When I get a chance, I inquire about it on social media. I have to tell you an incident, 'Magadi Sambhrama' happened recently. We, who were shooting, reached the event at 9 PM. The flower I had worn in the morning had withered, so I had taken it off. At that time, Sudha-Swara had not worn flowers. Girls look beautiful when they wear flowers. So they brought flowers they had tied themselves and put them in my hair. That made me very happy. I always wear flowers in my character. It shows how much people respond to the character and how subtly they observe. Similarly, they ask about sarees and dresses everywhere. Especially about the langa davani, they have inquired a lot. Not only that, my friends have also borrowed my dress for photo shoots.

How did you become an actress?

Mysore is our native place. My parents dreamed of me becoming an artist. I was in 4th grade. A small role in a Chandana serial. My father used to bring me from Mysore to Bangalore for shooting and take me back. My mother enrolled me in Bharatanatyam classes. During my degree studies, I was attracted to theater. I became known in a group called 'Rangakale'. That led me to Rangayana. I did many plays. I started learning direction there. A director who saw me at an event gave me an opportunity in a film. Then I got films one after another.

If you become a director, what would your first serial story be like?

I want to narrate a story that evokes curiosity. It should create thrill along with curiosity among the viewers. I wish to create a story that expands awareness through law and order, legal dialogue, and debates.

If you were to talk about your experience in the field of art...

Art does not favor everyone. If it does, it pulls you back to it, and I am an example of that. I was a talented student, a CA, and had a good job with a good income in a corporate company. Saraswati has brought me to serve art. I respect that and am continuing here. I have realized that patience is very important for those who wish to grow in the field of art.
