Father is the inspiration for daughter's film dream

Monisha

Vinayak K.S.

I have been interested in cinema since childhood. I used to go to film sets with my father. I used to skip school to watch filming. However, my family did not agree to me becoming an actress. After college, my dream of acting got the green light, said Monisha Vijayakumar, starting the conversation.

I had no interest in studies. I felt like I belonged to the film industry. But at home, education was given first priority. I got a degree in English and Theater at St. Joseph's College. After that, I studied screen acting for one year at the New York Film Academy. I learned acting by doing college plays and street plays, she explained about her acting preparation.

In this film, I appear in the role of Monisha. Acting in my first film under my father's direction brought both hesitation and excitement. My father's films are very close to reality. His belief is that the character should be woven in the same way we are. So he said that no preparation was needed for this role. My father's argument is that we should act by observing the life around us and gaining experiences from it. I appear in the film as a girl from an ordinary family. A love journey with Vinay Rajkumar also comes in the story. I have tried to do justice to my character as a girl from a poor family struggling to build a life in Bangalore, she says.

Since I haven't worked with other directors, I didn't know the difference between my father's film and other films. My father is very strict at work. He didn't give any concessions on set just because I was his daughter. He got the work done that needed to be done as an actress. Along with acting, I also worked in the direction team. I am learning all the work required to make a film. I had chosen writing as a subject in New York. So I am also interested in direction, she says.

When children of star actors come to the film industry, everyone's attention is on us. When we come as ordinary people, we can learn something through one or two films. But here, we have to prove ourselves in the first opportunity. It is well known that a second opportunity is difficult. Everyone says that we should uphold our father's name. This is a kind of responsibility. However, she believes that the pressure on girls is not as much as it is on the children of star actors when they become heroes.

My father is definitely my inspiration to become an actress. I grew up watching his acting. I have a strong desire to establish myself here as an actress. Direction is a different field. I haven't thought about it yet. I want to do roles that will make me recognized as an actress. I have heard some good stories. I will choose a story where the female character has importance, she said, pausing her speech. g
