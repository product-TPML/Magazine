‘iOS 27’ is Apple's highly anticipated operating system. This version, recently released globally, is available to eligible iPhone users. The company, which has packed it with features, has also upgraded it to suit today's AI era. The design and user interface have been refined to suit daily convenience.

New generation AI ‘Siri’

The main attraction of this version is the new form of the voice assistant ‘Siri’. For the new change, Apple has combined its own ‘Foundation Models’ and Google's ‘Gemini’ model technologies to make ‘Siri’ even more effective.

A separate app has been provided to chat with ‘Siri’ in a chatbot model and to review old chats. In addition, it analyzes your phone's calendar, gallery, mail, and messages, and provides accurate answers to daily questions.

Visual Intelligence

Visual Intelligence, a system that perceives digital information directly through the camera app, has been further improved. Even if you point the camera at any object, you can directly get the details of that object there.

You can point the camera at a hotel bill and share the bill amount with friends. You can scan the nutrition label on food and add it to the ‘Health’ app. You can scan an event poster or schedule and directly record the dates in the calendar.

Liquid Glass Interface Customization Apple has also made significant changes in the display design. Users are given a slider option to increase or decrease the transparency and frosted look of the liquid glass design as they wish. Also, there is an option to add extra large widgets to the home screen.

In the previous version, creating shortcuts was difficult. Now you can create the automation you want by typing it in a simple sentence with the help of Siri. For example: if you write 'Send a message to my family when I leave the office', the app will perform that task itself.

Updates in daily use

You can save a specific frame in the middle of a video directly as an image without taking a screenshot.

This filter helps to view photos taken by yourself in the gallery, excluding photos from WhatsApp or other apps.

Separate volume sliders are provided for alarms, ringtones, and system sounds.

iCloud Shared Albums support full-resolution sharing for photos and videos. Android and Windows users can easily exchange photos.

Giving priority to women, Apple has introduced a new section related to perimenopause and menopause in the cycle tracking section of its ‘Health’ app. In this, women can store their personal information. Supplementary information will also be available here.

Which

phones are available?

iOS 27 is available for iPhone 11 series and all subsequent models (iPhone 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18 series and SE 2nd/3rd Gen).

How to download?

On iPhone, go to Settings and click on the General option. There, if you press Software Update, another option called Download and Install will appear. If you click on it, the new version will be installed on your phones. Before that, it is mandatory to enter your phone passcode and agree to Apple's terms. Otherwise, use is impossible.
