Churning of the Ocean

The splendor of tradition and harmony

Complementing the front page, the articles by Manoj Kumar Guddi, Shashikant S. Shembelli and Srinivas Jokatte on 'The harbinger of harmonious tradition, Benaka who unites people, Mumbai Aala Re Aala...' introduce a different dimension of cultural background. These articles provide information on how the Ganesha festival is celebrated in different places.

–Gurulingappa E. Biradar, Vijayapura

The article about the Ganesha festival has turned out very well. In the current era of communal conflicts, this article is an eye-opener for people of all faiths. I was happy to learn about the Hindu-Muslim harmony in some places in North Karnataka.

–T.S. Nagaraja Rao

Gauri Putra, Ganesha, Vignavinashaka, is a power beyond the boundaries of caste, religion and language. The strategy formulated by Bal Gangadhar Tilak to unite Indians in the fight against the British still stands as the strong backbone of the country. Jai to Ganapa, the lover of modaka, who wears the durva grass that asks for no pomp and gives the desired fruit.

–Vijayakumari, Bengaluru

I read three articles. Recently, noise pollution due to the use of DJ and air pollution due to excessive bursting of crackers are increasing. Benaka himself needs to open everyone's eyes against such deadly things.

–Hurukadli Shivakumar, Bachigondanahalli

The front page article gave joy along with devotion. Our Benaka is the worshipper of all religions! I remembered those days when all the family members used to go to watch the Harikatha, Rasamanjari, and entertainment programs held in the evenings after installing Ganapa for a month in my hometown.

–T.S. Pratibha, Chitradurga

'Sudha' that came for Vigneshwara's festival was full of devotion and emotion. All three of its articles captivated the readers' minds.

–Lakshman Rao M.L., Davangere

After reading the article 'Mumbai Aala Re Aala Ganapati Aala', I felt that even though I visited my daughter's house in Mumbai many times, I never got to see the grandeur of the Mumbai Ganeshotsav.

–Prabhaprasad

The writings about Benaka and Benaka's own appeals are like a mirror held up to the present times. The public Ganeshotsavs, which were started with the noble intention of uniting the people of society, have lost their original purpose due to the pomp and show they have reached today. The procession, the blare of DJs and the dancing of intoxicated people are painful to see and hear.

–Vasanna, Dharwad

Real Heroine Priya

Pilot Priya has reached the pinnacle of achievement. Priya, who flies to rescue others risking her life, should be a role model. We, who become fans by seeing heroines in movies and wishing to be like them, should consider such celebrities in real life as true role models.

–Dhanalakshmi Purushottamarao
