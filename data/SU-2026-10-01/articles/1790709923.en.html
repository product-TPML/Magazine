INDIA

Health, education
should be priority areas...

It has been eight decades since India gained independence. During this period, our country has made significant achievements in various fields and developed in many sectors. However, from the perspective of the majority of ordinary citizens, many more tasks urgently need to be done. The primary needs of a healthy and civilized society are health and education. There is a need for radical and rapid change in these two major sectors. But both these sectors are becoming the property of private institutions, moving away from their original aspirations.

As days pass, these sectors, which are becoming unattainable for the common people and out of reach for the economically weaker sections, need to be considered a priority by the government. A blueprint for reform needs to be prepared, and all necessary resources need to be provided in a timely manner. The management of these sectors needs to be strengthened, accountability needs to be fixed, and the administrative system needs to be made transparent. The day must come when all public representatives and all government officials compulsorily use government educational institutions and government health centers. This is my dream for India.

---Manjunath D.S., Bengaluru

May India become heaven

My dream India means a country that shows unity in diversity. Here everyone gets education, health, and work. In this India, there will be no hunger, poverty, or caste-religion divides. The farmer's sweat will be valued. Women will have respect and safety. Children will go to school and dream of flying high. Science and culture will go hand in hand. A corruption-free administration, clean rivers, green forests, our country leading in technology – this is my dream.

The 'Swaraj' envisioned by Gandhiji, the 'Developed India' envisioned by Kalam - the day must come when all these become one. Not just dreaming, but every citizen must work honestly to make it a reality. As it is said, 'Jani Janmabhoomischa Swargadapi Gariyasi', our India should become our heaven.–A. Mrutyunjaya, Pandavapura

Everyone should become 'Indian'

The specialty of my country is 'unity in diversity'. In India, there are people of all castes, all languages, and all religions. Our constitution beautifully refers to it as a 'country of religious harmony'.

When there are hundreds of problems in the country, thousands of people are struggling for two meals a day, clothes to cover themselves, and a roof over their heads, those who are currently ruling us, the politicians, are trapping the people in the whirlpool of caste and religion to serve their own interests.

India can progress only if everyone practices their customs, beliefs, methods of worshipping God, and food habits within their homes, and when they come out, a law is enacted that everyone is an Indian. This is my dream India.

–Indira Vivek, Mysuru
