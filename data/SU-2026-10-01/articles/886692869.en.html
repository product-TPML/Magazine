Ambur Chicken Biryani

What do you need?

Chicken one kg, Sona Masoori rice one kg

Ginger-garlic paste one spoon, ginger paste two spoons

Garlic paste two spoons, coriander leaves one cup, mint leaves one cup

Byadagi chillies twenty, spicy chillies five, chilli powder one spoon

Garam masala one spoon, cloves six, cardamom two, black cardamom one

Peppercorns eight, cinnamon three pieces, Marathi moggu two, nutmeg a little

Star anise one, onions four, tomatoes four, bay leaves two

Curd two spoons, oil, ghee, salt

How to make?

Mix chicken with curd, ginger-garlic paste, garam masala, salt, chilli powder and refrigerate for two hours. In a pressure cooker, add a generous amount of oil and ghee, then add pepper, cinnamon, cloves, cardamom, black cardamom, bay leaves, star anise, Marathi moggu, and onions and fry. Then add garlic paste and fry. Then add ginger paste, tomatoes, coriander leaves, mint leaves, and ground dried chilli paste. Fry until it releases juice, then add chicken and salt and mix. After cooking for a short time, add slightly less than two measures of water, add the washed rice to this mixture, close the pressure cooker lid and cook.

Brain Curry

What do you need?

Three goat brains, ginger one inch, garlic a little

Coriander leaves a little, mint leaves a little

Kasuri methi one spoon, coriander powder one spoon

Chilli powder one spoon, turmeric powder a little, peppercorns five

Cloves three, cinnamon one inch, poppy seeds a little

Nutmeg a little, roasted gram a little

Onion one, tomato one

Coconut 1/4 piece, mustard, ghee, salt

How to make?

Grind onion, coriander leaves, mint, turmeric powder, ginger, garlic, tomato, coriander powder, chilli powder, coconut, nutmeg, roasted gram, poppy seeds, peppercorns, cloves, cinnamon with water. In a pan, add ghee, mustard, the ground ingredients and salt. Cook until oil separates, then add brain, kasuri methi, cover and cook.

Egg Chops

What do you need?

l 10 boiled eggs, ginger 2 inches, garlic 2 cloves, coriander 1 bunch

l Mint leaves 1 bunch, coriander powder 2 spoons, green chillies 6

l Turmeric powder a little, pepper powder a little, garam masala a little

l Chilli powder a little, onions 3, tomato 1

l Coconut 1/4 piece, mustard, kasuri methi 1 spoon, oil, ghee, salt.

How to make?

In a pan, add oil, turmeric powder, chilli powder and chopped boiled eggs and fry. Grind 2 onions, coriander, mint, ginger, garlic, pepper powder, coconut, garam masala, green chillies, tomato, coriander powder with a little water.

Add a little oil to the pan where eggs were fried. Add finely chopped onion, coriander, mint and kasuri methi with mustard and fry, then add the ground mixture. After frying well, add a little water, fried eggs and ghee, mix, cover and cook.

Egg Cashew Masala

What do you need?

6 boiled eggs, 5 tomatoes, 2 onions, 15 cashews

Ginger-garlic paste 2 spoons, turmeric powder a little

Garam masala 1 spoon, chilli powder 2 spoons

Coriander powder 1 spoon, kasuri methi 2 spoons

Mustard, oil, salt.

How to make?

In a pan, add oil, a little chilli powder, salt, turmeric powder and chopped boiled eggs and fry. In a pan, add oil, onions, tomatoes, green chillies, coriander, mint leaves, cashews, fry on low heat, then cool and grind finely. In a pan, add oil, finely chopped onion with mustard, kasuri methi, ginger-garlic paste, fry, then add turmeric powder, chilli powder, coriander powder, garam masala, salt, and after frying, add the ground mixture. After frying until oil separates, add eggs, cover and cook on low heat.

Finger Chicken

What do you need?

l Chicken (boneless) 1/2 kg, cumin powder a little, garam masala a little

l Pepper powder a little, coriander powder 1 spoon, chilli powder 3 spoons

l Maida flour 1 spoon, corn flour 2 spoons

l Ginger-garlic paste 1 spoon

l Bread crumbs 1 cup, chicken eggs 2

l Salt, oil, lemon 1

How to make?

To well-washed chicken, add maida flour, chilli powder, corn flour, ginger-garlic paste, cumin powder, garam masala, pepper powder, chicken eggs, salt, mix and refrigerate for half an hour. Roll chicken pieces in bread crumbs and deep fry in hot oil on medium heat. Then serve on a plate with onion, cucumber, and lemon juice.
