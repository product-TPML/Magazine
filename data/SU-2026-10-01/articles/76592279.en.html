‘Yours is a problem with a solution. Mine is a problem with no solution,’ she said. Those words gave me boundless enthusiasm. ‘Please tell me how,’ I said.

Just for outward appearance, very modern garments have arrived. If you want a permanent solution, silicone implant surgery. Many girls are getting it done recently.

‘Being so innocent, why did you agree to live together?’

‘‘He should lie on his stomach and cover his face with a pillow. I will write a letter on his back with my index finger. If he says it correctly, he wins..! ‘Is that all? Is everything done?’ I asked.

After asking that, I was surprised. A few days ago, I was someone who didn’t even like chatting about such things... When she talked about Shimla, I argued about moral values and restrictions... Why am I talking like this now? I thought.

I think when girls reach a certain age, they have both a fear of doing something and a curiosity to do it. Romance has that kind of power. I am not above this state.

Newton became a great scientist out of curiosity as to why the apple fell down. Some girls step into the mud out of curiosity to see what a new experience would be like.

I have a quote in my diary.

Experience is what helps humans in the journey of life, traveling from ignorance to knowledge.

If one understands the good in experience and uses it correctly, it becomes a bridge. If one enjoys the bad, thinking it is good, it becomes a whirlpool.

Whenever I said such quotations, she would get very excited. She would encourage me, saying, ‘Bravo, you collect them well.’ Once the dam is opened, the flood of words doesn’t stop, does it? We would tell each other so many stories. Most of them were about romance. She would tell her experiences. I would listen enthusiastically. Although I sometimes felt guilty, I would console myself, saying, ‘It’s just listening, isn’t it? It’s the influence of age.’

One day she sent me her photo. The part below the neck, uncovered. The strange thing was that this time I didn’t feel anything was wrong. I kept looking at that photo enthusiastically for a long time. We discussed it for a while.

‘By the way, did you ask your boyfriend about your breast surgery? What did he say?’

‘I’ll ask after we get married. If I ask now, he’ll slap me till my cheeks swell. He’ll inquire who’s telling me all this.’

‘By the way, I have good news for you. We’ll meet soon.’

As if I had shrieked, I asked, ‘Really? When?’

‘We are coming to your town next week. Tell me where and at what time to meet. It’s the rainy season, isn’t it? It will be easier if we plan beforehand. I’ll tell you the date before we leave,’ she said and hung up the phone.

Just the thought of Suvarnasundari meeting me made me feel very excited. In my mind, youthful feelings, mischievous age, secrets are shared only with one or two people, aren’t they?

‘What should we do on the evening of the day we meet? How should we spend it?’ After that, for four days, it was a constant discussion between us! As the day of Sundari’s arrival approached, it started feeling even more thrilling.

‘You’ve seen my photo, haven’t you? You’ll easily recognize me. Send your photo too. You didn’t send it no matter how many times I asked before, did you?’
Apologizing, I sent the photo. She replied, ‘You are very beautiful. The one who marries you is very lucky.’

‘What is mine compared to your beauty?’ I said. She was indeed very beautiful in the photo.

‘We are meeting, aren’t we? I’ll buy you a nice dress. When we go to the clothing store for that, let’s see who is beautiful in the mirror.’

‘How many days will you stay? Where will you stay?’ I asked.

‘I’m coming just for you. I’ll stay for one day. I’ll leave the next morning. I don’t have anyone there. I have to stay in some hotel.’

‘Oh! Why come for me and stay somewhere else? Stay in my room. We can chat all night.’

‘One bed. You’re in danger,’ she said mischievously.

‘It’s okay. I know how to protect myself,’ I said, holding the phone and going to the window. A cool breeze blew in. As I watched, spring passed and summer arrived. Storms and tsunamis are said to come during this season.

The next day, she called and told me the train time. ‘I’ll be in a white churidar. Do you have a green dress?’

‘Why?’

‘White is a symbol of peace. Green is a symbol of romance.’

‘Like two ascetics rubbing against each other, what romance will we two do?’ I said intimately.

‘If two ascetics rub against each other, ashes fall. If two girls rub against each other, news falls.’ She laughed and said, ‘Recently, there has been a change in girls’ thinking too, Arushi. Girls are making more friends with girls, wondering why there are ego clashes and disputes with boyfriends. Recently, lesbians have increased by twenty-five percent.’

Disliking this topic, I said, ‘Let’s meet tomorrow. Bye.’

‘Don’t sleep all night. Lie on your stomach and think about our meeting. Bye,’ she said mischievously and hung up the phone.

The next day, as I was going to pick her up, a call from Amritavarshini! ‘Come to the studio urgently. I need to take the camera and go somewhere,’ she said.

‘I can’t come. I’m going on a very important task,’ I said and hung up the phone.

Within two minutes, a call came from the boss. ‘Very important meeting. Varshini can’t handle it alone. You also have to go. You must be here in ten minutes,’ he said and hung up the phone without even waiting for my answer. I know what kind of person he is. If I don’t go, there will be a letter on my table the next day. And it was a permanent job just the other day.

All that was left was for me to cry. Crying in my mind, I went to the office and from there, taking the cameraman, I went to the shooting spot. Varshini didn’t come. I had already told you that she has a close relationship with the channel owner, hadn’t I? It meant she cleverly put her work on me...!

Even though I was working, my mind was all with Suvarnasundari. I called and told her what happened. She took it lightly and said, ‘How long? Just an hour, isn’t it? I’ll sit in the retiring room. Come to the station after your work is done.’

I thanked her in my mind. As soon as the work was done, without wasting a minute, I rushed to the station. She wasn’t in the retiring room. I called. She didn’t answer. I was distraught. I didn’t know what to do. I sat there for another half an hour, and since she didn’t appear, I came to my room.

An hour later, a call came from her. As if I was waiting for it, I quickly picked up and said, ‘Hello.’

‘I saw your missed call,’ said Suvarna.

‘That’s right. Where are you? Tell me that first,’ I shouted, distraught.

‘To tell you the truth? I didn’t come alone. I came with my boyfriend. We both thought we’d give you a pleasant surprise. There’s a famous pilgrimage site near your town, isn’t there? ‘You must send your breast photo, just like I sent mine.’

I should tell Prabhakar everything. It was truly my mistake not to have told him all this time. He is the only one who can free me from this problem. Since Prabhakar was already caught in Arushi’s case, if he was encountered while being arrested and taken away, no one would suspect anything.
My thoughts again turned to Arushi’s murder. Why did she get so distraught when the inspector was talking? Especially when the topic of nude photos came up.
