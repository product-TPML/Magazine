Vidisha Kallamundkur

Jewellery is no longer just an item to be worn on special occasions; it has become an important part of daily attire.

Fashion is also not limited to clothes, but is a personal style that reflects one's personality. For independent young women, it is also a means to represent their personality. To attract the youth, who are the focal point of the fashion world, new jewellery keeps entering the market every day. Especially in earrings, various new designs keep getting introduced.

Modern and unique designs are now the favorite choices of young women. Earlier, they were attracted to heavy gold jewellery. Now they prefer to wear light and comfortable jewellery.

Whether it's earrings, bracelets, necklaces or rings, their first priority is that they should be comfortable to wear. This is mandatory in the case of earrings. The first expectation when buying is that they should not be irritating when worn all day.

College, office, party, get-together... they prefer to wear different types of earrings for every occasion. Especially, they are fascinated by minimalistic, geometric or oxidized art earrings.

Earrings play an important role in enhancing the beauty of the face. Here is a glimpse of the best and trendy earring designs that young women must have in their collection.

Bow Earrings

Bow design jewellery has recently created a stir in the fashion world. These earrings, made of pearls, stones, fabric, ribbons and various metals, are available in different sizes. Their cuteness attracts young women. Although they seem like a very simple design, they give a good look. They are mostly designed in stud style, and they go wonderfully with Western outfits and make you stand out in a crowd.

Evergreen Floral Design

Floral design earrings have been in use since ancient times. Since young women are attracted to vintage fashion, various floral earrings are available in the market. They are trending on social media.

Art blooming in polymer clay:

Recently, these have been making a lot of noise, and earrings are designed using clay. Many types of floral artworks are made in this.

‌

The beauty of real flowers in resin: These earrings, made by processing real flowers in resin, look very attractive. This design is also very popular with the younger generation who love nature and care deeply about it.

Nature-inspired earrings: In this nature-inspired design, earrings are given the shape of hibiscus petals, orchids or palm leaves. These earrings, available in dangle earrings, studs, and large hoop designs, are perfect for a party or a beach trip. They are a perfect match for modern outfits.

Many options in metal: These, available in golden, silver and rose gold colors in metal, look very stylish. They match Western, Indian and most casual outfits.

‘Ring’ style according to the times

These earrings have been in use since the beginning. But their design has changed with time and has transformed into today's modern world.

Chunky, thick hoops, vibrant hoops, mini huggies, stacking, textured and geometric... these are the most trending ring earring designs on Instagram. They match all types of casual dresses. Nowadays, people use mini huggies and stacking more. Young women often wear hair buns for comfort. Ring earrings go well with this hairstyle, and also enhance the beauty. Bow, artistic floral earrings or rings, all these determine the confidence and mood of young women.

In today's fashion world, jewellery is designed in many ways, and there are hundreds of options to suit the tastes of young women. You too can add these trendy designs to your collection.g
