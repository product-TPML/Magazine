The crows living all over the country gathered that day. The leader of the crows, 'Kakaraya', presided over the meeting. As soon as the meeting began, thousands of young crows pleaded in unison, "Kakaraya, we are tired of this life. Living in human society, we are hurt by their insults and praises. Why do we face criticism and ridicule that no other birds do? We must meet Brahmadeva, who created us, and demand a solution to our problems."

Kakaraya, being a hot-blooded youth, was enraged upon hearing the problems of his subjects. He promised to present this problem to Brahmadeva, get a solution, and inform them in the next meeting. Encouraged by their leader's assurance, the crows cheered "Kaka... Kaka... Kaka..." and flew back to their villages.

Kakaraya undertook severe penance to please Brahmadeva. This penance, which lasted for many months, had cooled Kakaraya's hot blood. However, his resolve to question the injustice they faced from human society was strong, so Kakaraya did not give up his penance.

Seeing Kakaraya's determination and severe penance, Brahmadeva appeared. "My child Kakaraya, I am pleased with your penance. Tell me, what is your heart's desire?" he asked. Kakaraya replied, "My salutations to the Creator. O Lord, the pain and losses inflicted upon our crow lineage by humankind are immense. Despite all the pain and suffering, we strive for the betterment of humankind. But these humans do not speak a single good word about us. They insult us with the most derogatory terms. Why do we face this ridicule that no other birds do? We are tired of this crow birth. What is the solution to this?"

Brahmadeva smiled faintly and remained silent. Disturbed by this, Kakaraya said, "O Lord, are you not also responsible for our plight? A cuckoo comes and lays its eggs in our nest when we are not around. Later, its chicks eat, play, and grow with our other chicks. We feed those cuckoo chicks as if they were our own. We, who raise cuckoo chicks like our own, are considered harsh-singing birds in the eyes of the world. The cuckoo's voice is dear to everyone! Why did you give us such a flaw of forgetfulness?"

He continued, "In human society, if they see someone dark-skinned, they call them 'black as a crow'. If someone's voice is a bit harsh, they call it 'crow-throated'. If creditors pursue them, they say, 'You haunt me like a crow'. If someone disrupts a शुभकार्य (auspicious event), they call him 'Shani (Saturn) like a crow'. Thus, these humans, who blame us for every disorder and transgression, only expect us during Pitru Paksha (fortnight of ancestors). That is why this time, during Pitru Paksha, we have decided that no crow in any corner of the country should accept the pind (food offering) offered by humans."

After listening to the crow's lengthy request, Brahmadeva, with the same gentle smile, said, "Look, Kakaraya, everything you have said so far is literally true. The work you do is excellent for all time. But why did you develop the intention of expecting praise from humans? Look, Kakaraya, this human is a selfish creature. He cannot see your motherly heart, your environmental consciousness that prevents diseases by eating dead creatures, your intelligence, or your inner beauty. He is blind, without an inner eye. I do not understand why you or your lineage give so much importance to the words of such a weak-natured creature. Moreover, it is wrong to desire that someone should recognize or praise us for the work we do. That is a human trait, not yours. Perhaps, due to long association with humans, their weakness may have transferred to you. Understand this subtlety and continue your work."

Kakaraya attained enlightenment. As soon as he realized where the solution to the problem lay, he spread his wings without another word and flew into the sky.g
