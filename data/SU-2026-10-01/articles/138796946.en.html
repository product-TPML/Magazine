Rukmini becomes Mangalagowri

Rukmini-Malashree

Vinayaka KS

Actress Rukmini Vasanth, who recently starred in the film 'Toxic', is currently a much sought-after actress. She is acting with Nivin Pauly in Malayalam. She has also made her mark in the Tamil and Telugu film industries and has announced another film in Kannada.

Rukmini, who garnered attention in an historical role with her acting and costume in 'Kantara Chapter 1', appeared in a role quite opposite to that in 'Toxic: A Fairy Tale for Grown-Ups'. Now she is ready to appear on screen as 'Mangalagowri'. The first look poster of the film has been released, and Rukmini appears as a police constable.

Kannada film industry's dream girl Malashree has joined Rukmini in this film. Rahul P.K, who previously directed the film 'Sakutumba Saparivara', is directing this film. 'KVN Productions' and director Hemanth M. Rao's 'Dakshayani Talkies' are jointly producing the film.

“Actress Malashree will appear before the audience in a brand new avatar never seen before in this film, and she has appeared in a role that will appeal to today's young generation of viewers. Rukmini Vasanth will shine in a role of equal importance alongside Malashree,” said the film crew.

'Sapta Sagaradaache Ello' was the film that brought Rukmini popularity. The director of that film is the producer of this film. Also, the team that grew up under actor Rakshit Shetty is behind 'Mangalagowri'. Therefore, it is said that Rukmini has accepted this film among many others. g

Ragini Dwivedi in 'Bingo'

Actress Ragini Dwivedi currently has several films in hand. Her film 'Sarkari Nyayabale Angadi' was recently released. Now her film 'Bingo' is also ready for release.

R.K. Chandan is paired with her in the film. "My character will have two shades. I have acted in a role I have never done before. Lately, I have been accepting different roles. I am happy to be getting good roles. Recently, female characters are getting importance. I am happy to have worked with a good film team," said actress Ragini Dwivedi.

Raksha Nimbaragi has appeared as the heroine in the film directed by Shankar Konamanahalli. Ragini's film 'Sindhoori' is also in the shooting stage. She is the heroine opposite Dharma Keerthiraj in the film. She is also acting in Tamil and Telugu.  g
