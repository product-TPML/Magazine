Malli's Flower

Vani Mysore

Malli, who sold flowers near the Mahadeshwara temple, was very sad that day. There was no smile on her face. Instead, a painful emotion was dancing on her face. Since she was sitting without responding to customers, the flowers in the basket were wilting.

Malli, who used to laugh cheerfully and sell flowers quickly and go home, had lost interest in selling flowers today and was sitting dejectedly.

Rangi, who was selling flowers next to her, seeing Malli's condition, asked, 'Hey Malli, why are you so sad today? The flowers in the basket are not empty yet!'

Malli said, crying and crying.

'My daughter has left home. She has apparently married some rich boy. She doesn't want to see my face! She says she doesn't like me selling flowers on the street, or doing housework. She says I'm an embarrassment to her.

'Never come. Don't come looking for me and ruin my happiness,' she wrote in a letter and left.

Malli sobbed and wiped her tears with her sari pallu.

'This is the reward I got for selling flowers for her, scrubbing dishes in four houses, buying her the clothes she asked for, and educating her in the college she wanted...' Malli cried.

Rangi comforted her... 'Just wait, Malli, the hardships you have endured will surely be rewarded. Your daughter is now floating in a colorful world of riches. One day or another, she will realize her mistake and definitely come back. Until then, leave her alone. Let her be in her own way,' she said.

'From now on, live for yourself. No one is for anyone here,' she also advised.

Malli said, 'Yes... true.'

She took the flowers from the basket, offered them at the feet of the deity, and walked towards home with a heavy heart.
