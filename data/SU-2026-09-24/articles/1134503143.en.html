‘I became an actress again out of stubbornness’

Raghavendra K.

How did you develop an interest in acting?

My love for art was born from my brother's encouraging words. My father nurtured that desire. I had an interest in theater since childhood. As soon as I finished my degree, I wanted to be recognized as an artist. I discussed it with my parents and expressed my desire to go to Bangalore. They moved their house from Hosadurga to Bangalore for me. My father used to sell coconuts. After coming to Bangalore, the first thing I did was join a theater group called 'Kalaatmaka'. Along with that, I started giving auditions. I got my first opportunity in the serial 'Varalakshmi Stores'.

How challenging is it to portray comedic and tragic roles?

One has to immerse oneself in a role that evokes tragedy or pathos. Sometimes, we have to recall the pains we have seen and experienced in our lives. To bring such a character to life, I mentally prepare myself so that tears come naturally. I don't feel like I have acted in comedy so far.

What is your dream role that you absolutely want to do in life?

The role I want to do in life should show qualities that I don't possess as if I do. For example, it should be like the character played by Radhika Pandit in the movie 'Krishnan Love Story'. A dream role is to act as a normal person who is abnormal.

How do you feel about the character of Renuka?

It's a character I really like. She handles an emotional elder sister. On the other hand, she also manages the childish Nandini. The character has a wide scope.

How do you feel when you visit your village now?

When I go to my village, I receive a lot of respect and love from people. I feel happy that I have had the good fortune to experience all of that. My parents also express pride in me. My brother also played a big role in my growth. I will always remember that.

Initially, acting felt difficult. I had given up my efforts halfway, thinking it was an impossible task. I joined a private company. After working for six months, I started to feel that this was not my field. I started giving auditions again with the confidence that I should act again and that I could do it. The atmosphere here was also intimidating. The self-confidence to overcome that has made me an actress.

What kind of guidance do you offer to new artists?

It is as true that there is a lot of competition now as it is true that opportunities are easily available. Those who want to enter the field of acting for the first time know a lot of things. Many become popular through reels. There is a big difference between social media, movies, and serials. Acting must be learned. It is not enough to come with passion. It won't work if you come as a hobby. There must be a belief that this is a question of life and death. Only then is it possible to find a foothold here. g
