Niranjan's yearning for success

Vinayaka KS

Niranjan

Vinayaka K.S.

‘Preparations for the film's release are in full swing. The songs have already received a good response. Expectations for the film have increased. I have played the role of a journalist in the film. As a journalist who writes crime news, I go to places no one has visited. It's a film with love, commercial elements, and a family story. Abhiram is the name of my character,’ Niranjan began, sharing information about the film.

‘Starting with 'Bellary Naga', I have made four or five films. Every field has its ups and downs. We have to keep working and stay stable. The effort is continuous. I am constantly making films by choosing good teams. 'Spark' is a film that was completed and ready for release in a very short time in my career,’ he said.

I had a passion for cinema since childhood. I grew up watching my uncle Upendra. I prepared for cinema, received theater training, learned dance and fight. I have worked as an assistant director. I have experience in the film production department. I came step by step. I made a film with my aunt. Then I acted as a hero in a film. I appeared in a Telugu film, he recalled his cinematic journey.

Staying stable here is a challenge. One should not lose hope. It is not possible to expect success in the first film itself. Many films are needed to get closer to people. People also need time to see us and recognize our talent. My goal is to reach people and stay in their minds. He believes that this struggle will continue until a film becomes a hit and we establish ourselves.

One must understand what people want. Recent Kannada films are not connecting with the audience. I am trying to understand why. I am choosing stories that have themes that are close to them. We need films that the whole family can sit and watch. The story should create the hero. I believe that one should not make a story for the hero. He says that I should prove myself as a hero within the story.

Even with or without a family background, staying here is only possible with the blessings of the people. So, it doesn't matter which family I come from. We become actors only when people feel that this boy has some talent. We must leave our mark in our work. Currently, I am doing a film called 'Next Level' with Upendra. I have heard two more stories. But they are not finalized yet, he said, pausing his speech. g
