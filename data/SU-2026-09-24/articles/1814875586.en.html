Set Dosa

What do you need?

One cup of rice

Quarter cup of flattened rice

Two tablespoons of split black gram

One tablespoon of split chickpeas

Quarter teaspoon of fenugreek seeds

Half a bunch of dill leaves

Salt to taste

How to make?

Soak the rice and lentils in the morning. After soaking for four to five hours, drain the water, soak the flattened rice and add it to the mixture, then grind it finely. Then add the dill leaves and grind again in the mixer. Add salt to taste. Once the batter has fermented slightly, pour it onto a hot griddle in the shape of a set dosa and cook well on both sides. Serve hot with chutney. If desired, you can prepare the batter in the evening and make dosas in the morning.

Rava Uttappa

What do you need?

l One and a half cups of semolina, one cup of curd

l One cup of grated carrot

l Half a cup of chopped onion

l Green chilies to taste, one tablespoon of chopped ginger

l A little chopped coriander leaves or dill leaves

l Two sprigs of chopped curry leaves, salt to taste

How to make?

Add salt and curd to the semolina and let it rest for half an hour. Then add all the above ingredients and mix well to make a smooth batter. Then pour the batter onto a non-stick griddle in the shape of a dosa and cook well on both sides over medium heat. Serve hot with butter.

Sweet Corn Pulao

What do you need?

l One cup of sweet corn, one cup of pulao rice

l Half a cup of capsicum slices, one cup of milk

l Two to three green chilies, quarter cup of grated coconut

l Six cloves of garlic, one teaspoon of cumin seeds

l A handful of coriander leaves, one inch of ginger

l A little cinnamon, two cloves, two cardamoms

l Salt to taste, one tablespoon of lemon juice

l Three tablespoons of ghee for tempering, half a teaspoon of cumin seeds

l One bay leaf, half a cup of finely chopped onion

How to make?

Add cumin, green chilies, ginger, cinnamon, cloves, cardamom, coriander leaves, garlic to the grated coconut, add a little water and grind finely. Then add ghee to the pressure cooker, when hot, add cumin, onion, bay leaf and fry for one minute, add sweet corn and capsicum slices and fry for another minute. Add the ground spices and fry until the raw smell disappears. Add the washed rice and fry for another two minutes. Then add milk to it. Add enough water, mix salt and lemon juice, close the lid of the cooker and cook for three whistles.

Sweet Corn Enne Rotti

What do you need?

l One cup of sweet corn, one cup of sago

l Half a cup of rice flour, quarter cup of semolina

l A little chopped coriander leaves l A little finely chopped ginger, curry leaves

l Two chopped green chilies l Salt to taste, one tablespoon of lemon juice

l Oil for cooking

How to make?

Soak sago for three hours, drain the water, add all the above ingredients and mash and mix well. Add water only if needed. Adjust the consistency of the dough, flatten it gently with your hands on a banana leaf or butter paper, place it on a hot griddle, add oil on both sides and cook thoroughly. Serve hot with coconut chutney.

Sweet Corn Pakoda

What do you need?

One cup of sweet corn, half a cup of rice flour

Quarter cup of gram flour, one cup of thinly sliced onion

Quarter cup of chopped coriander leaves

A little chopped ginger, one tablespoon of red chili powder

Salt to taste, half a tablespoon of lemon juice, oil for frying

How to make?

Put sweet corn in a bowl and mash it well with your hands once. Then add all the remaining ingredients and mix well. Add water only if needed to adjust the consistency of the batter. Then heat oil in a pan. Once hot, drop small pakodas and fry until golden brown. When frying, cover the pan with a sieve. Sweet corn may burst. Alternatively, you can grind sweet corn coarsely without water. Serve hot with tomato sauce.
