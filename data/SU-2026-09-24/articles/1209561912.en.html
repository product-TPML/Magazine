From now on, AI is not just about chatting... it's a 'super assistant' that completes all your digital tasks in the background. 'Meta' has introduced a new type of AI technology called 'Muse' which has practically fulfilled such a possibility.

'Muse' takes us from the era of information-providing AIs to the era of 'autonomous AI agents' that independently perform tasks in the digital world. No matter how intelligent large language models like ChatGPT and Gemini are, technically their operation is only within the 'prompt-response' framework. Meta Muse is a 'Personal AI Agent' technology that goes beyond this framework to make autonomous decisions and complete complex tasks.

Journey towards Agentic AI

Current AI models are called 'Generative AI' because they 'create' text, images, or code by analyzing data. They do not have the 'ability to work independently' in the physical or digital world. Muse AI is not just a language model, it is a 'Large Action Model' (LAM), meaning it not only reads websites but also manipulates web pages like humans. It has the technical capability to click on options and fill out forms itself.

Background Operation Technology?

Normal AIs stop working as soon as the browser tab is closed. But Muse's analytical power lies in its background operation. This is called Asynchronous processing. Meta provides each user with a separate cloud-based virtual machine called 'Muse Secure VM'.

Even if you give instructions like 'Plan all travel for next week and book tickets', turn off the phone screen, and get involved in other tasks, Muse, through this virtual machine, independently continues the process of searching various airline websites, comparing prices, and booking tickets in the background.

Autonomy in Transactions

The biggest barrier for chatbots was financial transactions. From a banking security perspective, authorizing AIs to make payments is a challenge. Meta Muse has found a solution to this challenge through a security system called 'Sentinel'. Instead of using the user's real credit/debit card, Muse creates 'virtual cards' (Single-use virtual cards) limited to that specific transaction. It only compulsorily asks for the user's permission at the final stage of deducting money.

Error Resolution Capability

If the link you gave to a normal AI doesn't work, it shows an 'Error'. Muse uses logical thinking. For example, if a website's server is down while booking tickets, it automatically decides to go to an alternative website and complete the task.

'Muse' is a milestone in the history of artificial intelligence. It introduces us to an era where AI works as our digital representative, moving beyond the 'chatting' phase of talking with AI. In the coming days, the competition between technology companies will be decided not by 'which AI gives the best answer?' but by 'which AI works best?'.
