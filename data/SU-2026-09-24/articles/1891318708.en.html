A young woman studying B.Sc Psychology at a college in Kochi had to cut short her education a few years ago due to low attendance. Now, she is making waves as a popular actress in the film industry. She is twenty-five-year-old Mamitha Baiju. This entire year has been a golden period for Mamitha. The Tamil film 'Jananaayagan' was a super hit. The Telugu film 'Vishwanathan and Sons' also achieved success at the box office. Now, the Malayalam film 'Bethlehem Kudumba Unit' continues its winning streak in earnings. Vijay is the hero in the first film. Surya in the second. Nivin Pauly in the third. All these films together have earned more than seven hundred crore rupees.

At a time when the pan-India film mantra is failing, the success of 'Vishwanathan and Sons' and 'Bethlehem Kudumba...', which embody local flavor, serves as a lesson for filmmakers.

Bethlehem director-screenwriter Girish A.D. had recognized Mamitha's talent years ago. He gave her significant roles in the Malayalam films 'Super Sharanya' and 'Premalu'. In the film 'Premalu', the actress's emotional performance and the confidence required for the role received widespread praise.

Mamitha also acted in the Tamil film 'Dude', which was successful last year. The number of Gen-Z followers of this actress has increased. This is also proving to be conducive to film marketing.

Recently, during the Onam festival, Mamitha was supposed to be the chief guest at an event in Australia. She had to cancel the trip due to severe fever and body aches. As soon as she posted this on social media, the number of people wishing her a speedy recovery began to rise. This simple post also contributed to the increase in her social media followers.

When Telugu director Venky Atluri narrated the script of 'Vishwanathan and Sons', veteran actress Radhika Sarathkumar had said that the heroine's role was substantial and required a talented actress to handle it. Later, when she saw Mamitha in that role on set, she was amazed. Her spontaneous exclamation, 'What a wonderful artist,' was a certificate for this actress.

When Mamitha's birth certificate was being written, an office assistant made a spelling error, writing 'Mamitha' instead of 'Namitha'. Later, the girl's parents decided to keep the same name. Some people are commenting on social media that this changed name also changed the actress's future. g

'Varanasi' VFX work speeds up

The Telugu film team of 'Varanasi', which started shooting last year, has completed 90% of the scenes and is smiling with confidence.

The film, starring Mahesh Babu, Priyanka Chopra, and Prithviraj Sukumaran in lead roles, is directed by S.S. Rajamouli. After the shooting reached its final stage, the VFX work has picked up pace.

Mahesh Babu plays the character of Rudra, Priyanka Chopra as Mandakini, and Prithviraj as the antagonist named Kumbhi. There is an opinion that Rajamouli is attempting to tell the story of Rama and Kumbhakarna from the Ramayana in a time-traveler format.

Prithviraj has praised Rajamouli's working style, where he gathers opinions from all actors and actresses before shooting each scene and conducts healthy discussions. Rajamouli has promised, 'This time I will release the film on time.' g
