Sahana, a bank employee married for three years, yearned for a child. Her husband, who works in a private company, did not have much desire for a child, which led to a lack of understanding between them. Finally, after convincing her husband to try for a baby, the result was zero. Disappointed, Sahana went to a doctor, and the gynecologist suggested IVF with an advanced method to conceive.

For various reasons, couples today are not conceiving naturally. For such couples, IVF (In Vitro Fertilization) is the only suitable method to see the smile of a child at home, according to experts.

‘The matter of having a child is not entirely dependent on age alone. Generally, the age group of 20 to 35 years is the best for conception. Among these, around 30 is the ideal time to conceive. However, this depends on factors such as the woman's overall body health, ovarian reserve, the husband's health status, polycystic ovarian syndrome, or weight. Especially after the age of 35, the quality of eggs produced in the ovaries tends to decrease. Nevertheless, it is entirely possible to have a healthy baby through medical examination and appropriate treatment,’ says Dr. Devika Gunasheela, an IVF specialist at Gunasheela Fertility Center in Basavanagudi, Bengaluru.

‘Due to changed lifestyles, late marriages, postponing thoughts of having a child, stressful lives, consumption of fast food, lack of walking exercise, etc., couples today are delaying having children. For some women, conceiving is becoming impossible. For such couples, IVF is the only suitable path,’ advises this doctor.

What are the tips for IVF success?

After thirty, natural conception is usually rare. Couples who marry late need to completely change their lifestyle to have a child. A blood test called 'AMH' (Anti-Mullerian Hormone), which shows how many eggs are left in the ovary at the right time, should be done. Tests such as hemoglobin, vitamin D, TSH (thyroid) should be done as soon as IVF is considered. Men need to undergo sperm tests and sperm count tests.

‘In India, the problem of infertility is worsening day by day. The number of couples undergoing IVF treatment is also significantly increasing. IVF centers are also increasing. The first IVF center in India started in 1986. Now thousands of IVF centers are operating across the country,’ explains Dr. Devika.

According to the recent National Family Health Survey (NFHS-5), 27.5 million couples in India are seeking treatment for infertility. An estimated 15-20% of couples are experiencing fertility problems due to lifestyle issues. According to a study conducted by the All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS), the sperm count in men has significantly decreased. This is also a reason for the worsening problem of infertility.

‘As age increases, the quality of eggs from the ovary decreases. This leads to a higher possibility of not conceiving, miscarriage, and congenital problems like 'Down Syndrome' (intellectual disability),’ she says.

Down Syndrome Statistics

At age 25

Approximately 1 in 1,000 to 1,300 pregnancies is estimated to have this problem.

At age 30

Approximately 1 in 650 to 1,000 pregnancies may show this problem.

By age 35

This ratio changes, and 1 in 350 to 400 pregnancies may face this problem.

Upon reaching age 40

The risk level increases, and there is a possibility of 1 in 70 to 100 pregnancies having this problem.

By age 45

The risk increases sharply, and approximately 1 in 30 pregnancies may show this problem.

There is no need to panic just because of increasing age. In today's modern reproductive medical world, a state-of-the-art genetic test called PGT-A (Preimplantation Genetic Testing for Aneuploidy) is available. Through this, by testing the quality of embryos at the IVF stage and transferring only healthy embryos to the uterus, a healthy baby can be obtained. At the IVF treatment stage, before transferring the embryos to the uterus, the chromosomes in the embryo are thoroughly examined through this special test. Only completely healthy and defect-free embryos are selected and implanted in the uterus. This significantly increases the possibility of a healthy baby being born even in older individuals.
