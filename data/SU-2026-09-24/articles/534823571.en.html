Formal shoes for the office, sneakers for going out, slippers for home or a nearby shop... once upon a time, men's footwear choices were limited to these. That has now changed. If you observe the footwear men wear in coffee shops, restaurants, airports, malls, or offices, that change is evident. They wear sneakers with formal pants, loafers with jeans, and sandals with linen pants. Many also wear running shoes while traveling. Earlier, there was a calculation of one shoe for one occasion. But now, they choose footwear according to comfort, occasion, and style.

In 2026, men's shoe fashion has changed significantly. It's not possible to say that only one type of shoe is trending. "There is a high demand for comfortable, versatile, and simply designed shoes. Some old-style shoes have also come back into trend," says Rawoof, who has been selling shoes for many years at City Center in Gandhinagar.

In the fashion of slippers...

Slippers were used in ancient Egypt, Mesopotamia, and Indus Valley civilizations. They were made from materials found around them, including leather, plant fibers, and papyrus (a plant that grows in marshy areas). Their main purpose then was to protect the feet from hot ground, stones, and dust. Over time, they ceased to be just foot protectors and became a reflection of a person's profession, social status, and culture.

In India, diverse footwear has been in use since ancient times. There were, and still are, different types of footwear like common slippers, Kolhapuri, Jutti, Mojari. With the industrial revolution, there was a big change in the manufacturing of footwear. At the same time, sports shoes also became popular. Shoes made for various sports including running, tennis, basketball, and football crossed the stadium and became part of everyday attire.

The unwritten rule that specific types of shoes should only be worn for specific occasions has now been broken by current fashion. The "old rules" about shoe usage are now changing.

Sneakers: From the gym to the office

Once primarily used for sports or exercise, sneakers are now being used in offices, for travel, shopping, and meeting friends. Behind this is not just fashion, but also a change in work culture. Hybrid work, smart casual dress codes, long commutes, and a lifestyle where most of the day is spent outside – all these have increased the demand for comfortable footwear.

Supporting this, RedSeer's 2026 consumer study also presented interesting facts. Two out of three Gen-Z individuals prefer semi-formal or casual wear in the workplace. The use of fashion accessories among Gen-Z youth has doubled, and the number of sneaker users has also increased. The study states that athleisure sales have also doubled.

Shoes should look good and be comfortable all day long. They should be wearable when going to the office, traveling, or meeting friends. It's even more convenient if one pair of shoes can be used from morning until returning home at night. The fact that one shoe should adapt to multiple occasions has increased the demand for sneakers. Sneakers are now part of not only the gym but also the office, coffee shops, and travel.

From chunky to low-profile

A few years ago, chunky sneakers with thick soles and large shapes were popular. Now, along with them, low-profile sneakers are trending. These shoes, resembling 1970s and 1980s tennis and running shoes, have thin soles and a small shape. They go well with many outfits, including jeans and casual pants. That's why they are a favorite shoe for fashion lovers. According to estimates by the International Market Analysis, Research, and Consulting (IMARC) organization, low-top models accounted for 43% of the sneaker market in India in 2025. Lightweight design, comfort, and versatility are the main reasons for the demand for these models.

It's not that chunky sneakers are not in demand. They have their own customers. Running, performance, and retro sneakers are also available in the market.

Loafers: Up to smart casual

The popularity of loafers in men's footwear is increasing. The main reason for this is that loafers easily adapt to formal and casual wear. Oxford and Derby shoes are still suitable for weddings, formal events, and offices with strict dress codes. The rule of wearing formal shoes to the office every day is not as prevalent now. Hence, loafers are becoming more popular.

Leather or suede loafers can be worn with many types of pants, such as jeans, chinos, linen pants, or formal trousers.

Increased demand for domestic brands

"As the sneaker market grows in India, the number of domestic brands has also increased. Earlier, there was a perception that sneakers meant foreign brands. Now, consumers are showing a preference for Indian brands. Along with price, people also look at how they look, if they are comfortable when worn, and how long they last. This is increasing the demand for Indian brands," said Adeeb, a shoe seller at Bajaj Mall, Gandhinagar.

There is an increasing demand from youth for sneakers ranging from ₹3,000 to ₹7,000. In the Indian sneaker market, domestic brands are also being chosen according to budget, style, and need.

Color change in footwear

Men used to feel that choosing black or white shoes was safe. That has now changed. They are choosing cream, beige, tan, olive, brown, and chocolate colored shoes.

These colors easily match linen, cotton, and denim outfits. Especially with casual wear, they look more natural. Some prefer simple colored shoes, while others want their shoes to stand out among their clothes. Thus, various colors are available in men's shoe fashion.

Sandals are back in fashion

Sandals, once used for indoor and casual wear, are now appearing in new forms. In place of common rubber slippers, leather sandals, fisherman sandals, woven slides, double-strap sandals, and well-designed flip-flops are available in the market. In recent fashion trends, there is an increasing inclination towards fisherman sandals, woven slides, and premium flip-flops. Open footwear is suitable for India's sun and climate. Now, good design has been added to that comfort. Sandals made from various materials including leather and woven materials are coming out. They can be worn for travel, holidays, with casual, and ethnic wear.
