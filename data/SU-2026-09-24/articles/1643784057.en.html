Teacher: Why are you late?

Student: I was watching a World Cup match in my dream, sir...

Teacher: What's the connection between that and being late?

Employee: The match went into extra time, sir!

***

Father: If you pass the exam, I'll buy you a bike. If you fail, what should I do?

Son: What's the big deal, Dad? Buy me an auto... I'll drive it and make a living!

***

Patient: Doctor, I have a sleepwalking problem at night, what should I do?

Doctor: Before you sleep, lock the room door and throw the key out the window.

***

Ramu: Today at the office, the manager saw my work and said, 'Wow! Super!'

Somu: Tell me the truth, what did he say?

Ramu: 'I've never seen such an utter flop work in my life, wow! Super!' he said!

***

Doctor: Your right leg is older than your left leg.

Patient: How is that possible, Doctor? Both legs were born on the same day, weren't they?

Doctor: Yes. Every time you come to the clinic, you enter with your right leg, that's why I said that.

***

Father: Hey son, if you fail the exam, don't call me 'Dad'.

After the exam results came out...

Father: What were the results, son?

Son: Who are you, sir?

***

Harshita: What would you do if you won 50 lakh rupees in the lottery?

Harsh: I'd immediately give half of that money to you.

Harshita: Are you serious? 25 lakh rupees for me? Can I do whatever I want with it?

Harsh: Yes, I'm telling the truth.

Harshita: Oh... you're so good. I misunderstood and made a mistake.

Harsh: Why, what happened?

Harshita: I thought you wouldn't give me a single rupee from the winning money, so I tore up the ticket and threw it away.

Harsh: Never mind, let it go. That ticket didn't win anyway.

Harshita: Look here (showing the ticket number) 50 lakh rupees came for that ticket number!

***

Police: Breaking into the house at 2 AM, what were you doing there?

Thief: Sir, the door was closed, so I knocked. No one opened it. I got scared that something might have happened to someone inside, so I broke the door and went in.

Police: Oh! Then why did you carefully take the cash and jewelry and put it in your pocket?

Thief: I kept it safe, sir, so thieves wouldn't come and steal it!

***

Wife: What gift do you want for your birthday?

Husband: Just give me something valuable that is silent and peaceful.

Wife: Okay, I'll sit next to you and keep my mouth shut for a whole day.

Husband: Wow! I never thought you'd give such a valuable gift.

***

Teacher: Ganesha, where does the sun rise?

Ganesha: It rises in our window!

Teacher: (Angrily) Are you being mischievous?

Ganesha: It's true, sir. As soon as the sun comes up in the morning, my mother scolds me, 'Hey Ganesha, wake up, the sun has come through the window and you're still sleeping!'

***

Friend: Gunda, you bought a new phone, why did you immerse it in water?

Gunda: The shopkeeper told me this phone is 'waterproof' and has an excellent 'liquid display'. So I'm checking it by putting it in water.

***

Customer: Sir, a fly has fallen into this hot coffee.

Waiter: So what now? Will that fly drink all the coffee in the cup? No, right? Pick it out and drink it.

***

Vijaya: I cleared my home loan.

Harsha: Oh, then throw a party.

Vijaya: The loan is cleared, and the account is empty.

Harsha: What's the big deal? Just take another loan.

***

Lawyer: Manja, you break into people's houses and steal, aren't you scared?

Manja: Why should I be scared, sir? Before breaking into a house, I fold my hands and say, 'God, may this work finish well. A share is reserved for you too,' and then I go inside.

***

Doctor: To control blood pressure in the body, you need to walk at least 5 km a day.

Gunda: Okay, Doctor, I'll meet you after two years.

Doctor: Why after two years?

Gunda: If it's 5 kilometers a day, that's 1825 kilometers a year. By then, I would have reached Jaipur, more or less, from Bangalore. I have to come back from there, don't I?
