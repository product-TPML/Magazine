May civic consciousness grow...

We are behind in punctuality. So, we are always in a hurry when driving. Civic consciousness is very weak. Even if we clean the road in front of our house by pouring water, we keep throwing garbage here and there. We are satisfied if our dog dirties someone else's house. When building a house, exceeding the rules by building more floors, not leaving setbacks, not leaving space for vehicle parking and obstructing others by parking on the road. It is truly strange that those who go to parks for walking, exercise, and fresh air use vehicles and cause air pollution. Due to unnecessary use of vehicles, the import of crude oil increases and our rupee keeps falling against the dollar.

How wonderful it would be if governments stopped indiscriminately cutting down trees in the name of development, and stopped conducting token Vanamahotsavas and then forgetting about them!

Just like in olden times, lakes in all towns need to be revived. Then rainwater can be held there. No one can object to the rain that falls in our state. There would be no disputes over river water flowing from one state to another.Many years ago, there used to be a 'Moral Science' class in high schools. Understanding moral values is essential for a healthy society. In all organizations, all employees should work honestly without any temptations. All these should be in my India.

--- B.N. Bharath

May it be a nation the world trusts

My dream India doesn't have to be new; it should be excellent in its eternal values. This sacred land, which has shown the path of peace, coexistence, and knowledge to the world for thousands of years, should become the light of the world. Its progress should not only be reflected in tall buildings or strong economic statistics, but also in the character of every citizen.

'Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam', 'Atithi Devo Bhava', 'Satyameva Jayate' are not just our mottos; they should become the values of every Indian's life. May India reach new heights in science, research, literature, art, sports, and economic achievements; may the roots of humanity, love for nature, and culture never dry up.

The sweat of the farmer, the sacrifice of the soldier, the knowledge of the teacher, the labor of the worker, and the honor of women should receive equal value. Honesty, sensitivity, and social responsibility should also become the benchmarks of national progress. May corruption, caste, religion, language, and regional differences melt away, and duty become the true form of patriotism.

The world should respect India as a beacon of humanity. Reaching new peaks of achievement in every field, it should become a role model for the world. Every citizen should not just proudly say 'This country is mine'; they should live with the responsibility that 'This country needs me'. More than conquering the world, earning the trust of the world is India's true victory.

--- Chinnaganahalli Suryaprakash
