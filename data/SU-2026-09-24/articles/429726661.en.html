Counseling solution...
'Where are we going wrong as parents?' Sahana Priyadarshini's article is truly an eye-opener for parents. Comparison with other children in reading and writing is not appropriate. Children should be encouraged to learn what they are interested in. While parents struggle to give time to their children, the burden of marks is another problem. When my elder sister failed SSLC, both my mother and grandmother consoled her saying, 'Learn tailoring, music. Do kitchen work if possible. Read books you like. Take the supplementary exam and pass. Why are you crying for that?' Even now, when exam results come out, I remember these words.
–Su. Vijayalakshmi, Shivamogga
