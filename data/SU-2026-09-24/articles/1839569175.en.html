Little Shambhu, as soon as he came home from school every day, would fill a bowl with chips, Kurkure, and other junk food, go to the balcony, sit down, and eat. His mother would tell him, 'Don't just eat junk food, I'll make you something good to eat,' but Shambhu would insist on chips, not listening to her.

Eating junk food every day, Shambhu's health deteriorated. He was always suffering from stomach aches. He didn't even have the energy to play with his friends. Seeing this, his mother was very sad. But what could she do, Shambhu never listened to his mother.

One day, Shambhu was sitting on the balcony, making a crunching sound and eating chips. There was a big mango tree next to the balcony. Two tiny squirrel babies on that tree heard the crunching sound. Curious about what this sound was, they scurried along the branch and peeked into the balcony. Seeing Shambhu eating chips, their mouths watered. Shambhu saw the squirrel babies looking at him, called them, and gave them some chips.

The squirrel babies ate the chips Shambhu gave them with a crunch, and they really liked this new snack. After that, they would wait for Shambhu to come to the balcony every day and would come and sit at the tip of the tree branch. Shambhu would give them chips and lovingly named these squirrel babies Chinni and Minni. This is how Shambhu and the squirrels Chinni and Minni became friends. They would eat chips and play with Shambhu.

Eating junk food with Shambhu every day, Chinni and Minni swelled up like balloons. Seeing her children suddenly get so fat, the mother squirrel became suspicious. When she waited to see what her children were doing, she found out that they were going to Shambhu and eating chips every day.

The next day, Shambhu came to the balcony with chips. Chinni and Minni were nowhere to be seen. Instead, a big squirrel came towards Shambhu, glaring angrily. Shambhu was scared seeing it. The squirrel said, 'Hey Shambhu, I am Chinni Minni's mother. Not only are you ruining yourself by eating these chips and junk every day, but you are also feeding them to my children and ruining them? Chinni and Minni have become lazy like you. If any enemies come, they won't be able to run quickly. If this continues, they will surely fall into the mouth of a cat or a dog. From now on, stop giving that junk food to my children. Otherwise, I will not let them play with you,' she scolded loudly.

Hearing the squirrel's words, Shambhu started to cry. He went to his mother, crying. When his mother asked what happened, he told her that the squirrel had scolded him. Then his mother said, 'Look Shambhu, you don't listen when I tell you not to eat junk food. The mother squirrel was right. At least now, stop eating junk food. Otherwise, Chinni and Minni will not come to play with you anymore.'

Shambhu also felt his mother's words were right. He didn't want to lose Chinni and Minni's friendship either. 'Okay, Mom, from now on I will stop eating this junk food and only eat the healthy food you give me, okay?' he said. His mother happily kissed him, saying, 'My smart Shambhu.'

The next day, as soon as Shambhu came home from school, he filled a bowl with fruit pieces his mother gave him and came to the balcony. Chinni and Minni's mother was guarding them on the branch so they wouldn't come near Shambhu. Then Shambhu showed the fruit pieces in his hand and told the squirrel mother, 'Look, squirrel mother, I have stopped eating junk food. I will eat only the good food my mother gives me. Please, will you let Chinni and Minni play with me?' he asked.

'Okay. Then Chinni and Minni will come to play with you. You are smart children, eat good food,' she said. Hearing what their mother said, Chinni and Minni, who were hiding behind the branch, happily chirped and ran towards Shambhu. Shambhu gave fruits to Chinni and Minni and ate some himself.

From that day on, Shambhu started bringing healthy fruits, vegetables, and nuts that his mother gave him every day and shared them with Chinni and Minni. Chinni and Minni became active and ran around like before. Shambhu's health also improved, and he became strong. After that, Chinni, Minni, and Shambhu, all three, were good friends, running and playing actively under the tree.
