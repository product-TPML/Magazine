The Lankesh family has a long association with the film industry. This journey, which began with Lankesh, continues into the third generation. Lankesh was known for his direction and acting. His son Indrajit and daughter Kavitha Lankesh also made a name for themselves as directors. Last year, Indrajit's son Samarjit Lankesh debuted as a hero in the film industry. Now, Kavitha Lankesh's daughter Isha Lankesh has entered the film industry as a heroine.

The film crew recently announced that Isha Lankesh is acting as the heroine in a new film directed by Preetham Gubbi. Sumukha, who acted in the films 'Physics Teacher' and 'Manada Kadalu', is the hero of this film.

'I am already active in theater and have prepared for this film,' said Isha. The film crew recently shared a video of the hero and heroine preparing for their roles.

Preetham Gubbi's previous film 'Baanadariyalli' did not achieve the expected success. Therefore, this is a significant film for Preetham Gubbi as well. Director Preetham Gubbi said that 'information about the film's title, as well as the rest of the cast and crew, will be provided soon.'

Malavika Sharma in Brundavihari

‘Brundavihari’ is a film made by the same team after the success of ‘Krishnam Pranayasakhi’. Actor Ganesh had said a few days ago that he has high hopes for this film. Devika Bhatt is paired with Ganesh in the film. Malavika Sharma has also joined them.

This film, directed by Srinivasaraju of ‘Krishnam Pranaya Sakhi’ fame, is releasing on October 16, and the fourth song of the film, ‘Nadasunada’, has been released. Malavika Sharma has danced in that song. The film crew said that she appears as Meera in the film. Devika Bhatt is Radha.

The film ‘Krishnam Pranaya Sakhi’ was successful because of its songs. The film crew has adopted the same strategy here. The film has a total of seven songs, and ‘Rama Rama Deva’, ‘Jeeva Jeeva’ and ‘Radha Radha’ songs have already been released and have attracted listeners.

This is the second film coming out in the combination of Ganesh and Srinivasaraju. All the songs have been written by V. Nagendra Prasad. Hesham Abdul Wahab has composed the music for the film. g
