Chidananda Sali, Art: Santhosh Sasihithlu

A mischievous young woman
Went to fetch firewood
Forgot the firewood, climbed a tree
And slept in the shade of the leaves

Another mischievous young woman
Came looking for dry leaves
Under the same banyan tree
She walked, trampling dry leaves

The rustling sound of dry leaves
Brought mustachioed cockroaches scurrying
The young woman, scared by the cockroach's mischief
Before returning to the sand dune

The dry leaves underfoot slipped with a rustle;
The cotton, blown by the wind,
Entered her nostril
She sneezed, slipped, and fell to the ground

The young woman on the banyan tree
Fell from the tree at the sound of the sneeze
They fell on top of each other
Rolled and rolled, then got up themselves

They walked, looking, laughing, and blushing
She at her, she at her;
Including dry leaves, cotton, and firewood

Miss

The language of cats is meow
The language of dogs is bow-wow

That of crows is caw-caw
All sparrows chirp chirp

Not just animals and birds
Insects too are understood

But Miss's mind is never understood
They are different every moment

Everyone says 'Keep Quiet'
But they teach while talking!

Chicken

Wake up, wake up, wake up
It's morning, wake up!

'Listen to the elders
Live to be great'
Says the chicken
Listen to the chicken's words!

Children who learn in school
Will shine in palaces;
Children who go to work
At the age of learning in school
Will remain laborers!!

Ant

Ant, ant, how are you?
Where are you going in a line?

You are like a baby elephant
You are walking looking for grain

How do you smile even when tired?
Don't you have any boredom?

Without bothering about the world
I forget boredom in work

I spend the whole rainy season at home
How do I stay without getting fat?

Be it rain or cold
I consume moderate food

More talk is useless, my friend
I am going to work

You also follow this method
Then you will also be slim

Children of Rain

Wind blew with a whoosh
Lightning flashed with a gleam
Thunder rumbled with a boom
Rain came with a patter!

Like a snake, slithering
Came home quickly
Mud, mud, water, water
The whole village sparkling!

Water pouring into the boat
Green crops swaying
A rainbow appeared in the sky
Colors enchanting to the eye!

My sister and I together
Made a sand house
Floated a boat
Danced with joy!

With a hundred eyes
Painted with a million colors
Becoming peacocks, dancing
We became children of the rain!

Shorty

Idli chutney
She wants

Get ready

Are enough!
