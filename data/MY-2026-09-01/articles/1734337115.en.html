The interview with folk scholar and researcher Prof. B.A. Viveka Rai (Kodibettu Rajalakshmi) published in July Mayura is worth collecting for several reasons. D. Ramanamali, Harapanahalli

The depiction of the reality of everyday life in the story 'Behind the Flower' (S. Seshadri Kinara) has come out beautifully. The pursuit of the girl Kusuma and the hardships are expressed humorously. Each joyful moment of life gives pleasure to the readers in the form of a story.

S.N. Kumaraswamy

H.V. Sanjay's poem 'The Poet's Day' was very meaningful. Similarly, M.S. Ashadevi's introduction of the hitherto unknown storyteller Tulasi Venugopal seemed like a useful work. Kodibettu Rajalakshmi has collected and documented a lot of information from the senior Kannada scholar Prof. B.A. Viveka Rai by interviewing him.

A. Raghuram, Bengaluru

H.V. Sanjay's poem 'The Poet's Day' captivated the mind. The poet's last day's reminiscence, the unreturning metaphors, and their details shone heart-touchingly in the lines of the poem. The description that when the poet reached the last moments of his life, it was as if he composed the last line of a poem of love for life, felt intimate.

K.P. Ramagundi, Bailhongal,
S.R. Narasimha Prasad, Kunigal

Kuvempu's first play 'Modannana Tamma', though small in size, holds as much importance as a full-fledged play due to its poetic dialogues, deep thought, and artistic expression. A. Raghuram's article, which meaningfully analyzed such an important work and effectively unveiled its subject, intention, and artistry, is truly remarkable.

Prof. Shivanna Belavadi, Tumakuru
