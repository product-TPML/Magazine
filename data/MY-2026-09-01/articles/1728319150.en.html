We for Tomorrow

paddy field.jpg

Image: Ram

Rain poured
Earth blossomed
Planting is tomorrow

Toil became a song

Song became life
A narrative descended to earth


Paddy planting

For unquenchable thirst

Will it be a match?


For the beauty of the land
Even in front of the house
Planting of the homeless


For the unbowed head
For the bent back
The burden of hope
The empathy of planting

For today, which exists and yet doesn't
For an unseen tomorrow, we
Will definitely be there

– Savitri Mujumdar, Koppal

Earth Song

Not far away
A small tiled
House
Surrounded by greenery
A verdant goddess

Blowing in
Gentle breeze
Not in coconut fronds
Tuning the strings
Playful swaying


Somewhere nearby
The flow of rain
The fragrance of the earth
In the paddy fields
The croaking of frogs
The sideways movement of crabs
In their burrows

On their heads
Colorful cloths
Turban
The joy of
Planting
Humming rain songs
No fatigue felt
By body and mind
If there is a resolve that
Work is Kailasa
Life is green
The sargam of life...

– Dr. Sudha V.R.
