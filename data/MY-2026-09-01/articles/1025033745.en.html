Flowing under time

The blue river

Is not just a source;

It is a water-memory song

Carrying silence.

As the blue of the river deepens

Sorrow finds an infinite color of compassion.

Compassion is

A long remembrance of water

That has dissolved the shadows of a thousand sorrows

Into light.

From there, an eye

Sees sorrow...

Even if there are a thousand rain wounds

On the heart of stone,

The river does not question them;

It keeps turning the echo that rises within it

Like a Dharma Chakra.

***

A stone

That fell from the temple wall

Becomes a symbol of labor.

Inside, the river

Flows silently

Between the stones without a word.

Sweat

Changes the expanse of the ocean

With its own salt.

A defiled hand

Caresses a hungry stomach

And shines like a Linga;

The step called caste

Becomes a cradle of time's dust,

And words

Speaking the language of sweat

Sit on the edge of the food pot

And share the bread of truth.

***

Inside the closed window

Stale air spreads

Following man's footsteps -

Like the shadow of caste.

It is

The silence of time

Left out of the light.

A blue river

Burns the ancient trees of history

And flows like black ink.

Here it

Translates the signature of blood

Into the language of rights;

Writing the struggles of breath

On the history of blood,

Time begins to read its own

Chains.

***

No liberation

Is complete with meditation.

No equality

Is complete with labor.

No justice

Is eternal in a book.

They

Must be born again and again

In the blood, in the sweat,

Of every generation.

Then

The Bodhi tree sheds its leaves

On the Ishtalinga.

The Ishtalinga

Unravels its circle

With blue letters.

Blue letters

Again

Plant the Bodhi tree

In the human heart.

From there

Dharma

Breathes as compassion, and work

As prayer.

The Constitution

Stamps in silence

That humanity is the first language.
