A Glimpse of Fire

ashok hosamani.jpg

Ashok Hosamani

Art:

Your
abstract
pages
caught fire
Which stream of love
of tiny anklets
can it reach


In the fiery, strong stride
of the silent cry of the soul
that is to be read


Every drop
of blood
from the dripping
pillar
as it swallows layers of fire
touching the clear light of a yawning day


The wells of fire
have burst open
along the rivers
of deep void
munching on flower babies
kissing haunting abstract pages
remembering
the deep dark pits of dried-up streams of love


In a glimpse
of fire
where can I hide
the ash-covered eyelids
in which chest
the tiny anklets that whispered
in defiance of the stream of love
in the eagerness of pages of void and non-void
