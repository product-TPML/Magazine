Mounesh Navalahalli Art:

The churning stick of labor

To play in the body

A necklace of dew drops all over the body

A pure mind

Adorned with righteous deeds

Sweat is not sweat, it's light for the whole world

A pot of food

A golden fan

For the bond between men and women

The bow of the workers' hands

The staff of righteousness

The source of knowledge for saints and sages

Voluntary service brings peace

Meditation on Shiva, voluntary service

The roaring fortresses of sweat

The ministers' presentation

The palace of the wicked

The strength of the common people is a mountain

Churning nectar

Working while consuming poison

The blue throat is a stepping stone for the body

For the veins of the world

A lamp of blood

Wherever sweat flows, there is greenery
