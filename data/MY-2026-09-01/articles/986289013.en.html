Urvashi's Disappointment

She had never experienced such humiliation. She was tormented by disappointment, anger, and shame. It was different when she failed to disrupt the penance of the sages. There, the intention was different. It would have been a task assigned by Devendra for the welfare of the gods. But this was also her desire. It was with the desire to enjoy herself to her heart's content, to lose herself in lovemaking for a night with the handsome man she desired, that she went to that special inner chamber. Yes. She had gone to Arjuna as an abhisarika, as per Devendra's instruction.

Chitrasena had said: 'Urvashi, Arjuna is our guest in Devaloka. You too have noticed that when you dance, he watches you intently, captivated. Arjuna has lost his heart to your enchanting and unique beauty. Spend a night with him and give him complete sexual pleasure.'

Dhananjaya, the friend of Sriramana Narayana. He performed penance, fought with Shiva in the guise of a hunter, pleased him, and obtained the Pashupatastra. Then he received the desired boon. Goddess Parvati gave him the Anjanastra. Thus, Arjuna became known as the hero of the three worlds. Devendra sent a chariot with Matali and summoned his son to Devaloka. He called him, honored him, seated him on the divine throne, and crowned him. Dhananjaya earned the title of Kireeti. In honor of his son Arjuna, Urvashi, Menaka, Rambha, Ghritachi, Tilottama, and others performed an enchanting dance performance. Oh! What a celebration!

The celestial maiden Urvashi, seeing the handsome celestial Arjuna, desired to make love with him. Arjuna too was gazing at her intently. He was captivated by her beauty. As if knowing this, Devendra wished to bestow heavenly pleasure upon Arjuna. Urvashi's bread had fallen into the ghee. She stroked her eyebrows. She applied kohl to her eyes. She wore a half-open bodice that made her breasts appear very beautiful. A simple dress and garland suited it. Beautiful women naturally look beautiful. What if they adorn themselves with suitable decorations? How much more beautiful would they look?

Her companions and other apsaras teased her: Urvashi, how beautiful you are! Now, any man who sees your enchanting form will surely lose consciousness. Your sidelong glance. The jingle of your anklets. Thighs that make the eyes of those who gaze upon them unable to move. Full, pointed breasts that tickle the chin. Full cheeks that one mistakes for a bee's flower. Now, with shyness, your cheeks have turned rosy! The radiance of your body can pierce the darkness. Your captivating eyes can shatter the hearts of sages. The pleasure of your body, which was not obtained by Nalakuvara, Varuna, or Jayanta, who stood in line desiring you, is now being obtained by Kireeti. How fortunate he is! They praised her beauty and teased her.

Urvashi's mind was thrilled. All night long, playful conversations; the union of two bodies. Unique sexual pleasure. Lovemaking. Remembering it again and again, she was filled with goosebumps and, burdened by shyness, she entered the sleeping chamber within Arjuna's inner apartment as night fell.

Enthusiasm and joy overflowed in her mind and body.

Arjuna saw the beautiful celestial maiden entering. For a moment, he gazed at her without blinking. He was surprised! The enchantress Urvashi stood before him. Her sidelong glance, like stars suddenly sparkling, captivated him. Ah, his mind too was disturbed for a moment. The next moment, he composed himself and spoke humbly: "Devi, please come and sit. Please accept the greetings of this young man. What service can I offer you?" he asked. Hearing the phrase 'greetings of this young man', Urvashi's face became somewhat pale.

She sat near him on the couch and replied:

'Is it not time for bed? Did the beauty of heaven bring joy to your mind? I have come now, as per Devendra's command, to spend the night with you. Urvashi, who has never been subdued by anyone all night, is now yours. A celestial maiden who has come to bestow heavenly pleasure,' she said with a gentle smile. She gently approached him, sat close to him, and lovingly stroked his strong arms.

Arjuna suddenly moved away and spoke in a trembling voice: "Mother, you are the queen of my father Devendra. How can there be union with a mother? Moreover, you are the beloved of Pururava, the ornament of our lineage. You are my paternal grandmother. You are venerable. Not to be enjoyed; a goddess of fortune to be enjoyed on the couch..." Arjuna stopped halfway.

Urvashi clarified: 'That is a matter of the human world. In our divine world, there is no such emotional bond. There are no restrictions on the pleasure of union. We are eternally youthful. No union defiles us. We go to earth for our recreation. It is a change of state. Enjoy me. Satisfy your desires. No sin will touch you. I will grant you a boon. I am a young maiden. Do I look old to be called a paternal grandmother? We have neither old age nor death,' she said, requesting sexual pleasure.

No, Dhananjaya did not agree to any of her words. He was not swayed by her charm and allure. He ignored her. Now Urvashi became angry. The neglect that poured cold water on the heat of her desire turned her into a burning ember.

Yes, no one had ever humiliated her like this. She was rejected. Her mind was enraged. All her joy and thrill melted away.

Her face darkened as if the moon had been eclipsed. Did the perfumes she had applied emit a foul odor? Did all the decorations she had put on seem like misdecorations? It made her feel unbearable.

She cried out in despair and humiliation. 'Oh Arjuna, you have ignored my desire. You have misinterpreted natural pleasure. You are not Kali Arjuna; you are Kapi Arjuna. Am I a beggar? You, who are called the husband of the three worlds, would be rightly called impotent. It is not even possible to say that. Because you have impregnated many beautiful women. You speak such big words, don't you? Are you any less in having relations with women you see? You desired the Naga maiden Ulupi and gave her a child. From there, you came to Manipur, charmed Chitrangada, and married her. You left your son Babhruvahana in her lap and went to Dwarka. Arjuna, the ascetic, there you seduced Subhadra. Now you preach philosophy to me, don't you? You should be ashamed. You kicked away the fortune of lovemaking that came to you. You humiliated me, who desired you. I will leave only after giving you a suitable punishment. I curse you to become a eunuch,' she said angrily and prepared to leave.

Arjuna immediately became alert, bowed to her, and said, 'Devi, this is not right. Do not curse me. I am a guest in your world. Is this proper?' he asked humbly.

For a long time, Urvashi stood silently, and her divine quality awakened. Slowly, her anger began to subside. She transformed her desire for lovemaking into love and compassion. Was there a lack of sexual pleasure for her? Urvashi's anger subsided, and she became calm. 'Alright, you are our guest. I am giving you a counter-curse. You will be a eunuch for only one year. That will be beneficial to you later during your period of incognito exile. You will become Brihannala and teach Bharatanatyam in Virata city. Let the curse serve as a boon,' she said with generosity, left the inner chamber, and quickly walked towards her palace. Arjuna stood motionless, watching.
