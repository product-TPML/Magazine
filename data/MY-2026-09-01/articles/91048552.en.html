Teaching is God

Under the weight of teachings,
devotion itself is changing,
the religions of Dwapara Yuga
are disappearing
in the clamor of Kali Yuga.


Giving breath to stone,
man became God,
but at the door of life,
the prayers of hungry eyes
are still awaiting an answer.


Who is the God the world saw?
He who saw the world's pain is God.
He who steals the heart
at the cry of the inner voice is God.


Urulu Seve, holy bath,
Aarti, the fair of Prasad,
amidst all this,
humanity, standing silently,
is disappearing.


Amidst the clamoring instruments saying
"Make way,"
the silent God asks,
"Where is the compassion within you?"

The eyes do not get cataracts,
cataracts come
to the eye of the mind.
That's why the devotee's tears ask,
"Does this God have no eyes?
Does he not see our troubles?"


Then the wind answers,
"God is not in the stone,
he is in the hand that wipes away tears.
Teaching is God,
humanity is worship."
