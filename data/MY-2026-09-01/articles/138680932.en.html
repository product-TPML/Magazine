A.K. Ramanujan

1

Before words end
God who stopped speaking,

Shouldn't you teach words,
Shouldn't you teach natural expression?

At least in matters of love
Shouldn't you touch the mind
Like touching the body
After losing all sense of propriety
And irony
In sarcasm?

For a minute
Show the nature of emotion.

2

The language of poetry is different

I don't reply to letters. Even if I do,
I don't post them. Even if I post them,
Many times they don't reach the person
Who wrote them. Even if they do,
They don't read it completely. They have
Other things to do. Even if they read it,
What I said was one thing, what they understood
Was another. This leads to misunderstandings.
The same happens when I receive letters from others.
That's why I don't reply to letters.

The language of poetry is different.


If it gets fixed,
If misunderstood,
It feels like a kind of meaning,
If meaninglessness, futility, and meaningfulness are felt in poetry,
All those are meaningless mines of stones and earth
The meaning found in them
Different techniques to filter it.
Everything is capable. Even lies are true.
Word itself is substance.
In its soil, a speck of gold.
In the stone, a vein of silver.
Even in the mud, a yellow eye struck by a chisel
A diamond waiting to be carved
How was Lanka burnt? When someone asked about history,
Tenali Rama burnt their house 'like this!'
And showed them,
And amidst that smoke, ash, and lamentation,
He said, 'Look here,
This is the true Ramayana,'
He showed it,
Like that.

3

Sh-Kindergarten? Oh, leave it.
The teachers of grandfather's time
Upside down
Made them do 'kodanda'
In chili smoke

T-The lesson taught was different

Varamahalakshmi Vrata, you say?
Oh, leave it. In the golden age of Vijayanagara,
Barren women hooked themselves to their private parts
And swung on a swing

The boon received was different

American film festival in Kerala, you say? Oh, leave it.
In Andhra, Harijans who touched the well of the upper caste
Were tied to a गोबरली tree with their families

The Y-Yaga (sacrifice) burnt was different

D-Daughter made a new lampshade?


M-Made? Oh, leave it.
Like the Nazis, they looked at caste
Boiled and peeled human skin
If a lampshade was made, it

The light it shows is different.
