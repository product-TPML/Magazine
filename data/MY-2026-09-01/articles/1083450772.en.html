Veerappa Moily

M. Veerappa Moily, Art:

Precious humor arose there on the horizon
A new joy on the face of the Supreme Being
A natural smile beyond words
Is there life before death
Such a Yaksha Prashne!

Achieve control with determination, conquer the mind
If you conquer yourself, there is no hint of defeat
If the control of life is within you
Is there life before death
Such a Yaksha Prashne!

Tear down the walls of problems that arise
You will see the beautiful ocean of life
The horizon of life will open there
Is there life before death
Such a Yaksha Prashne!

Behold the great path of the highest position!
The great plan to break through the enemy's obstruction!
Ahead is the victory your mind will achieve
Is there life before death
Such a Yaksha Prashne!

A heap of bad qualities has accumulated here
When will you break open the shell of ego and fall?
Then great knowledge will flow within you
Is there life before death
Such a Yaksha Prashne!

Let firmness be stable, abandon doubt
It will harm your mind, it will torment you
Break the fortress of doubt, move forward
Is there life before death
Such a Yaksha Prashne!

When you are the master of all means
When your body, mind, and the reins of the world are within you
When life shines and thrives
Is there life before death
Such a Yaksha Prashne!

Without being disheartened by defeat, knowing defeat in life
Overcoming conflict, thousands of defeats on the head
The elixir of success will flash
Is there life before death
Such a Yaksha Prashne!
