Did not perform 'Mangalarati'!

As soon as I entered the house with a milk packet in the morning, my wife said, 'The lady who recently moved in next door, I performed 'Mangalarati' for her early in the morning!' and after presenting her heroic tale, she went to the kitchen with the milk packet.

The previous night, during the puja ceremony at home, my sister-in-law's son, little Srinidhi, after the puja, had given everyone 'Mangalarati' along with 'Teerthaprasada'. Hearing my wife's 'Mangalarati' talk, he took me to the prayer room, showed me the 'Mangalarati' plate from the night's puja, and said, 'Auntie spoke in a loud voice to the lady next door, but she definitely did not perform 'Mangalarati'. I was there too.' he said.

For a few days, garbage was being dumped next to our house; my wife had stood hidden behind the window to see whose work it was; that day she found out who it was and that's how it happened! My sister-in-law's son really thought it was 'Mangalarati'!

Nagar Gurudev Bhandarkar

Headache

It was Sunday, and we were sitting and chatting, including my son, daughter, daughter-in-law, and my four-year-old grandson Agastya. Then my daughter told my grandson Agastya, 'Appi, my head is aching, please bring me Zandu Balm.' He brought the Zandu Balm.

My daughter started applying Zandu Balm to her forehead. Then my grandson Agastya came to me and said, 'Grandpa, Auntie doesn't know what's a forehead and what's a head. She's applying Zandu Balm to her forehead instead of her head when she has a headache. She has no sense at all,' and he left.

Everyone sitting there was surprised. Yes, we had to ask ourselves why we apply Zandu Balm to our forehead when we have a headache.

Bidarahalli Channaveerappa

Attained Knowledge

One day, Sheeni woke up in the morning, sat with his eyes closed in a spiritual mood, and asked himself in his mind, 'Who am I? Where did I come from? Why did I come? Where should I go?'

Just then, his wife Pammi from the kitchen shouted loudly, 'You are the number one lazy person. I don't know which world you came from. It seems you came to ruin my life. Get up and go take a bath quickly!'

Sheeni, who heard his wife's words, thought to himself that he had now attained knowledge and got up and left.

Aravind G. Joshi

Name it

The other day, Shyamalatte had gone to her daughter-in-law's house in Bangalore. As it became afternoon, mice started running in her stomach. She told her daughter-in-law Geetha, "Let's make mudde and rice, name it."

Geetha went to the kitchen and did not come out for a long time.

When her mother-in-law went to see, Geetha was thinking, with rice on one side of the two gas burners and water on the other.

When her mother-in-law saw this and tapped her head and asked, Geetha said, "Nothing, mother-in-law, I was thinking what to name the rice and what to name the mudde."

Actually, "name it" in rural areas means to prepare, to make (to put water to make rice, mudde). Geetha, realizing the truth, laughed at her own foolishness.

Deepashree S. Kudligi
